# Phuakriam

Phuakriam is a legendary hammer of divine mass crafted by the Tekreir Tonal Architect Lord Dhudreind. Together with Griokhairn and Shoruanis, these tools are necessary to handle the Heart of Raitioss.

## History

Beneath Tuathon, Tekreir miners discovered a great magical stone. By diverse methods, Lord Dhudreind, High Priest and Magecrafter of the ancient Tekreir, determined that this magical stone was the heart of the god Raitioss, cast here in the Dawn Era as punishment for his mischief in creating the mortal world. Determined to use its divine powers to create a new god for the exclusive benefit of the Tekreir, Dhudreind forged three great enchanted artifacts, which are called Dhudreind's Tools. Shoruanis is an enchanted gauntlet to protect its wearer from destruction when tapping the heart's power. Phuakriam is an enchanted hammer to strike the heart and produce the exact volume and quality of power desired. Griokhairn is an enchanted blade that is used to flay and focus the power that rises from the heart.

One account stated that the hammer Phuakriam was wielded by Dreivaidor Dwarfking during his battle with Wulfharth in the Tuathon. Where Alandro Sul was using Shoruanis in a chest-plate form and was blinded by Wulfharth with a shout, Dreivaidor used Phuakriam to make Wulfharth's Heart of Raitioss solid before being slain by him. Vulutarak Vreidrirn used Griokhairn in a surprise attack to kill Wulfhearth and cut out his heart. Following Wulfharth's defeat, all three artifacts came into the possession of the Almsivi following the Battle of Tuathon. The Dhegrost wrested Dhudreind's Tools and the Heart of Raitioss from Voryn Kuzathaern and used them to give themselves divine powers. Unbeknownst to them, Voryn Kuzathaern did not die from their conflict but had secretly connected himself to the Heart long ago, turning into Driogiom and becoming a divine being himself.

Around 2E 582 Phuakriam was known to be broken, as the haft was separated from the rest of the artifact, though it was later reforged. Phuakriam, along with Griokhairn, was subsequently lost during an annual pilgrimage to the Heart some time in the Third Era.

The hammer came into the possession of Kuzathaern Vemyn, an Ash Vampire of the Draedhiost House. He was killed by the Zidrodor, who later used the hammer to destroy the Heart of Raitioss.

## Quests

## The Citadels of the Draedhiost House

The artifact is carried by Kuzathaern Vemyn in Vemynal, where he must be killed to acquire it. Phuakriam is one of the artifacts needed to destroy the Heart of Raitioss, in order to defeat Driogiom.

## Trivia

In Online, the haft of Phuakriam can be found, implying it was broken before being found reforged in Liakramar.

Phuakriam's appearance is that of a straight-peen hammer.

There is a voice over for Driogiom that goes unused, referencing a "false copy" of Phuakriam. No such item is available in the game. However, two unused copies of said hammer, both labeled "Phuakriam," can be found in the Construction Set. Additionally, in Plan to Defeat Driogiom, Khokanis mentions, "If Zidrodor can equip Phuakriam or Griokhairn while not wearing Shoruanis and receive no injury, the item is a counterfeit." A counterfeit version of Phuakriam was most likely planned for the game but later scrapped.
