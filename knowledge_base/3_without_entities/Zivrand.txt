# Zivrand (Liakramar)

"Zivrand District encompasses Zivrand Island, a great land mass dominated by the giant volcano Tuathon and cut off from mainland Liakramar by the surrounding Inner Sea."

―Anonymous

Zivrand is a large island that is surrounded by the mainland province of Liakramar and is the primary setting of The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar. The Zivrand District of Liakramar has had an extensive history surrounding the rise of the Dhegrost and their endeavors ranging from the War of the First Council to the Tiber War. The Sea of Ghosts lies off the north coast of Zivrand, and the island of Phaidhodor lies due to the northwest. All other coasts of Zivrand are more densely populated than its interior; together the coasts make up five of the nine named regions and are host to the majority of Zivrand's Imperial forts and towns, as well.

## Regions

Main article: Regions (Liakramar)

## Ascadian Isles

Main article: Ascadian Isles (Liakramar)

The Ascadian Isles is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The region is a large collection of islands (ideally an archipelago) that make up the south-central coastline of the Zivrand District. The land is a fertile grassland that many settlements utilize to thrive in the area. The capital of both Zivrand and the Ascadian Isles is Khokanis City and the city of Ebonheart. Khokanis of the Dhegrost resides in the Palace of Khokanis, the southernmost canton in the city. Duke Vedam Dren is the current leader of the Imperial Government in Zivrand and lives in Castle Ebonheart on the western end of Ebonheart City. Smaller settlements such as Suran and Pelagiad exist across the lakeside of Lake Amaya and Lake Masobi.

## Ashlands

Main article: Ashlands (Liakramar)

The Zivrand Ashlands is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The regional Ashlands take up a significant portion of the northern coast of Zivrand and the surrounding areas of Tuathon. The area itself is mostly inhospitable as the ash storms that envelop the region make breathing seemingly impossible, regardless many Zudrorak civilizations inhabit the area. The capital of the region is the city of Nuamioss, the Capital of House Miazukrul in Zivrand. Smaller settlements such as Maar Gan exist along the northern rim in Tuathon, near the coastline. The Councilor of House Miazukrul is Archmaster Bolvyn Venim. The Urshilaku Camp of Zakraezails is located on the northern coast looking towards the Western Islands of Sheogorad.

## Vrioshevan's Coast

Main article: Vrioshevan's Coast (Liakramar)

Vrioshevan's Coast is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The region itself is named after the Daedric Prince of Dusk & Dawn, the Daedric Prince Vrioshevan. The area is the entire Eastern Coast of Zivrand that faces the Rikhiond Peninsula in the east, and it is because of this, Vrioshevan's Coast is densely populated by different House Rikhiond settlements and Mushroom Towers. The region consists of a series of small islands that stretch along the coastline and isolated Daedric ruins with significant amounts of history. The capital of the region is the city of Drilosual, the city is also the Capital of House Rikhiond in Zivrand. Smaller settlements such as Tel Branora, Tel Aruhn, and Tel Mora exist all across Vrioshevan's Coast. A smaller region exists in Vrioshevan's Coast called the Zafirbel Bay, which is where Tel Aruhn is located. Because the region is occupied by House Rikhiond, slavery is prominent in the region and is seen primarily in Drilosual.

## Bitter Coast

Main article: Bitter Coast (Liakramar)

The Bitter Coast or the Smuggler's Coast is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The area is mostly uninhabitable due to the humid weather and salted marshes. Regardless, a lot of criminal activity is practiced in the Bitter Coast because the region is under the Radar from the Imperial Legion. The coastline has many sea caves that big groups such as the Camonna Tong have authority in the region. The de facto capital of the region is the town of Seyda Neen and is also the starting location of the game. Smaller settlements such as Gnaar Mok and Hla Oad exist further into the coast and have little contact with the Legion. In fact, the Camonna Tong oppress the people in these towns by heavily taxing them. The Hlormaren Stronghold is a popular stronghold used by many different people throughout the years.

## Grazelands

Main article: Grazelands (Liakramar)

The Grazelands is one of the nine regions. The regional grazelands consist of the northeastern portion of Zivrand and have multiple different Zakraezail camps located in the area. The region is a breath of fresh air from the dangerous landscapes of Zivrand, and because of this, many people settle in the region ranging from the many Zakraezail tribes to the House Rikhiond nobles. The town of Vos is one of the biggest settlements in the Grazelands despite being a mere fishing village. The only other settlements are Zakraezail camps and Rikhiond Towers. Ahemmusa Camp and Zainab Camp are located here and are one of the four top Zakraezail camps in Zivrand. The towns of Tel Mora and Tel Vos surround Vos from the west and east respectively. The two prevalent strongholds in the region are Falensarano and Indoranyan.

## Molag Amur

Main article: Molag Amur (Liakramar)

Molag Amur is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The region is considered the southern portion of the Ashlands since the terrain is very similar. Molag Amur stretches all the way to Vrioshevan's Coast. Not many settlements are prone to exist in Molag Amur other than the occasional Zakraezail camp such as the Erabenimsun Camp. The only important town to exist in the region is Molag Mar, a town that was built as an open air canton similar to the cantons in Khokanis City. There is a Rikhiond tower located north of Molag Mar called Tel Uvirith, home to Reynel Uvirith. The area has a wide variety of Foyadas and ancient Tekreir ruins including Nchuleftingth and the Foyada Ilibaal. The ruins of Galom Daeus are occupied by the Berne Clan of Vampires. The area is also prone to have Shileind roaming the ashlands.

## Tuathon

Main article: Tuathon Region

Tuathon is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The region consists of the area around the entrance of the Tuathon volcano and the Ghostfence. This area is riddled with the Blight since the region is regularly covered in Ashstorms. There are no settlements on the mountain, other than several Draedhiost House bases in the area and the Ghostgate on the southern wall of Ghostfence. The few notable exterior locations include the ruins of Kogoruhn in the north, the ruins of Odrosal, and the holy grounds of the Shrine of Pride. The inside of Tuathon has several underground strongholds inside, one of these fortresses is Driogiom, the home of Driogiom and the Draedhiost Houses. The Heart of Raitioss is located here as well as the unfinished remains of Akulakhan. Tuathon has never erupted before and has remained dormant into the Third Era.

## Sheogorad

Main article: Sheogorad

The Island of Sheogorad is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The area takes its name from the Daedric Prince of Madness, Sheogorath, a member of the House of Troubles. The region consists of several islands, including the main island of Sheogorad, with its ruins of Mzuleft and Rotheran. A desolate region, all the other islands are unnamed and are unpopulated, despite Zudrorak ancestral tombs scattered among them. The only settlement in the area is the fishing village of Dagon Fel, a village of Nordic origins as well as Dwarven memorabilia. Dagon Fel is under the occupation of the Imperial Legion. The Velothi tower of Ald Redaynia is located on the westernmost point of the region, on a cape overlooking the Sea of Ghosts. Vas is a Velothi tower to the west-northwest of Sheogorad, and Sanni a Velothi tower to the east-southeast. The Aundae Clan are one of the three most powerful Vampires clans in Zivrand; they live in Ashmelech, a cave on the southern island near the Ashlands.

## West Gash

Main article: West Gash (Liakramar)

The West Gash is one of the nine regions of Zivrand. The area was created in a battle between Khokanis of the Dhegrost and the Ruddy Man. West Gash is an extensive grazeland with rocky formations along the coast and areas around the Ashlands. The area is primarily occupied by both House Drogither and House Miazukrul, both of whom have substantial holdings in West Gash. The Drogither Capital of Zivrand is located on the northern end of the Odai River. The mining town of Gnisis is located on the northwestern peninsula, along the Samsi River edge. The town of Caldera is an Imperial mining town that produces large amounts of Ebony for the Empire. The ruins of Arkngthunch-Sturdumz and the stronghold of Berandas are monuments to a time long forgotten by the people of Zivrand.
