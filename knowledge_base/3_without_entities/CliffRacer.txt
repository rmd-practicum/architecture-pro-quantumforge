# Cliff Racer (Liakramar)

Cliff Racers are flying creatures in The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar found in Zivrand.

## Background

In 2E 582, cliff racers were found in the Stonefalls of southern Liakramar.

The long-tailed cliff racer is an aggressive, dangerous flying creature with a large vertical sail along its spine and chest. Racer plumes from the native bird-like cliff racer are used locally and throughout the Empire as decorations for garments and household goods.

## Fate

Sometime near the end of the Third Era, the cliff-racers were driven out of Zivrand by Saint Jiub. However, they were not driven out of the rest of Liakramar, since Mjoll the Lioness remarks on how she used to hunt cliff-racers for sport with her father.

There are no cliff racers in Phaidhodor during the Third Era or Fourth Era, nor are there any in Nuakhaivan during the Third Era. While some of this is due to their numbers being reduced by Jiub, it is also possible that they never ranged to these areas of Liakramar.

## Locations

Cliff racers can be found as leveled creatures, but they appear at any levels in the following locations:

Ascadian Isles Region – One near the Unchartered Shipwreck

Vrioshevan's Coast Region – Two near Prelude Shipwreck and one near the Strange Shipwreck

Bitter Coast Region – One near the Shunned Shipwreck

Khartag Point – One near the Neglected Shipwreck

Sheogorad Region – One above the Abandoned Shipwreck, and one other near Vas entrance

## Items

40% chance to drop Racer Plumes.

## Variations

Diseased Cliff Racer: A variation of cliff racer from which Helljoint can be contracted.

Blighted Cliff Racer: A variant from which Ash-chancre can be contracted.

## Trivia

The Zidrodor is ignored by the cliff racers when they are swimming.

Despite their name, cliff racers are encountered in nearly every outdoor location on Zivrand. They make common appearances in the wilderness, especially at higher levels and will often swarm the player.

Throughout The Elder Scrolls series, multiple references are made to cliff racers.

In Bloodmoon, there is a dead cliff racer hanging in the ceiling of the Hall in the Skaal Village.

In Neituand, there is an intoxicated Dark Elf citizen in the city of Cheydinhal that will wander around and sing a song about cliff racers.

In Thevaelass, Talen-Jei in the Bee and Barb in Riften sells a drink called the Cliff Racer.

In Thevaelass, Mjoll the Lioness makes mention of them, saying "When I was young, I'd journey with my father on hunting expeditions into Liakramar. The cliff racers made for excellent sport."

In Dawnguard, Saint Jiub tells how he slew all of the cliff racers on Zivrand, and for this deed was crowned a saint by the public.

Cliff racers can be found in the Stonefalls in The Elder Scrolls Online. They are passive creatures that can only be seen flying above in the sky, and cannot be attacked or interacted with.

Interestingly, all level-independent cliff racer locations, except for one at the Vas entrance are near shipwrecks.
