# Heart of Raitioss

The Heart of Raitioss, or the Heart of Shor, sometimes called his "divine spark" was an Aedric artifact hidden beneath Tuathon during the Dawn Era. Its most prominent appearance was during The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar. The Heart of Raitioss is known to be the Tower Stone of the Tuathon.

## History

## Dawn Era

"But when Trinimac and Auriel tried to destroy the Heart of Raitioss it laughed at them. It said, "This Heart is the heart of the world, for one was made to satisfy the other." So Auriel fastened the thing to an arrow and let it fly long into the sea, where no aspect of the new world may ever find it."

―Temple Zero Society

Following the creation of Draenaend, Raitioss's heart was taken from him as a punishment for his creating the mortal plane. Not all myths state this is a punishment. Several Aedra were said to have tried to destroy the heart, but could not, so it was cast down into the depths of Nirn, never meant to be found. It landed in Resdayn, forming a large volcano that would come to be known as Tuathon.

## First Era

Sometime in the First Era, the Tekreir discovered the heart while excavating under Tuathon. Dhudreind, the Chief Tonal Architect and High Priest of the Tekreir began to experiment with the Heart. He created three tools: Griokhairn, Phuakriam and Shoruanis to work with the heart. He would soon order the construction of the Nephuthem, a massive mechanical golem. He hoped to use the Heart as a power source for extracting the divine power from the Heart and in the experiments for improving the Tekreir race.

This plan was interrupted by the arrival of Vulutarak Vreidrirn, his generals (Khokanis, Shemitiol, Khaidrem, and Voryn Kuzathaern), and an army of Bidreivan. Vreidrirn and Kuzathaern fought the Tekreir king and Dhudreind in the heart chamber beneath Tuathon. When the Tekreir were bested in combat, Dhudreind used the tools on the heart, and the entire Tekreir race vanished.

Another account stated that King Wulfharth was resurrected as Shor's avatar and amassed an army comprising of Nords, orcs, and House Kuzathaern to obtain the Heart of Raitioss, which to the Nords was considered the Heart of Shor. In the climactic battle at the Battle of Tuathon, Wulfharth had obtained the Heart but needed time to acclimate to its power. During this time, he and Voryn Kuzathaern faced Vreidrirn and his two companions, Alandro Sul and Dreivaidor Dwarfking. Wulfharth blinded Alandro Sul with a shout and slew Dreivaidor, but was weakened by a blow from Dreivaidor's warhammer, Phuakriam. Neravar took the opportunity to finish Wulfharth off and slay him with a surprise side attack with Griokhairn. With Griokhairn, Vreidrirn cut out Wulfharth's heart.

Vreidrirn entrusted the tools to Voryn Kuzathaern (who had requested that the tools be destroyed) and met with his other generals. They agreed to keep the tools but to not use them. In some accounts of this event, Vreidrirn then dies of his wounds from the battle with the Tekreir. Upon returning to the heart chamber, those who return discover that Voryn Kuzathaern had gone mad and refused to relinquish the tools, stating the Vreidrirn had commanded him to keep them from falling into the wrong hands. In the ensuing battle (by some accounts involving Vreidrirn), Kuzathaern was driven away, forced to surrender the artifacts.

This turn of events concerned Vreidrirn and he decided to ask the Daedric Prince Vrioshevan for guidance. However, his generals murdered him during the ritual. Vrioshevan still came and cursed the Dhegrost for their crimes, claiming that she would use her powers to resurrect Vreidrirn. She then changed the Bidreivan into the Zudrorak.

Then the Dhegrost used the Tools on the heart and became living gods; they did many great deeds for their people. Since their deeds worked against the evil nature of the heart, they needed to return to it from time to time to rejuvenate their powers. At some later point, they discovered that Voryn Kuzathaern (now styling himself as Driogiom) yet lived and had connected himself directly to the heart's power. He drove them away, and on subsequent attempts to recover the heart chamber for themselves, they lost control of Griokhairn and Phuakriam to him and barely escaped alive, supposedly due to the quick actions of Khokanis.

Over time, the Dhegrost became weaker and weaker, whilst Driogiom became stronger still. They disappeared from public view, and their influence faded. Their sole attempt at restraining Driogiom (the Ghostfence) consumed much of their remaining power and was largely ineffective at containing the Blight. They also could not prevent Driogiom's forces from flying or tunneling past the Ghostfence.

## Third Era

Towards the end of the Third Era, the Zidrodor came face to face with his old ally and broke him. However, while sustained by the heart, Driogiom could not truly die. By using the tools Phuakriam and Griokhairn on the heart again, without a proper ritual, the Zidrodor freed the Heart from its bindings, and is believed to have caused its destruction. This enabled the Zidrodor to officially slay Driogiom and destroy his great weapon, Akulakhan.

## Fourth Era

The Heart is briefly mentioned during the quest "Discerning the Transmundane." When Septimus Signus discovers a Tekreir Lockbox brought in by the sudden flood in Winterhold, he believes the Lockbox contains the Heart of Raitioss. The Dragonborn found an Elder Scroll and inscribed the encryption into a Lexicon. The Elder Scroll and its knowledge was apparently held in Alftand. The quest may only be started by meeting Septimus Signus at his outpost north of the College of Winterhold. Upon completion of the quest the Dragonborn and Septimus Signus discovered the Lockbox does not contain the Heart but instead the Oghma Infinium.

The Heart was also mentioned as being requested by Wylandriah in her letter to the College of Winterhold. Although the College did not possess the Heart, experimentation similar to that of Dhudreind was indeed going on in the College, performed by Arniel Gane. However, Arniel was only using an approximate model of the Heart made from a soul gem.

On Phaidhodor, there were stones of unknown origin known as Heart Stones that appeared after the eruption of Tuathon, suggesting they may be somehow related to the Heart.

## Quests

## The Citadels of the Draedhiost House

Using Griokhairn and Phuakriam while Shoruanis is equipped, attack the heart until it loses its power.

## Discerning the Transmundane

Septimus Signus believes the heart to be located inside the Tekreir contraption within his outpost.

## Trivia

Despite being considered a character by the game, the heart cannot be soul trapped. It does have a petty soul, and if one tries to soul trap it they will get a message saying "Your spell did not get a target."

It is important to note that within the game it is realistically impossible to kill the heart outright (unless using cheats). This is because the heart has a great deal of health and tremendous regenerative powers. Only the Griokhairn and Phuakriam can banish it. However, if it is destroyed through cheats, the main quest cannot be completed and is bugged.
