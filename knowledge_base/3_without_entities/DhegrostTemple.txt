# Dhegrost Temple

The Dhegrost Temple, commonly referred to simply as The Temple, is dedicated to the worship of the three god-kings of Liakramar known as the Dhegrost. The Dhegrost is composed of the goddess Shemitiol, the god-poet Khokanis, and Khaidrem. The three gained their divine power through their use of Dhudreind's tools upon the Heart of Raitioss, ultimately becoming the patron gods of the Zudrorak people. It was the dominant religion in the province of Liakramar, until after the events of the game The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar, where it was superseded by the New Temple.

## Beliefs

## The Anticipations

According to the temple the three Shileind that recognized the divinity of the Dhegrost were Vrioshevan, Boethiah, and Siavaes. These three Shileind are known as the "Anticipations" because they share the same aspects and spheres as each member of the Dhegrost. Each Shileind is an anticipation for each of the Tribune; Vrioshevan is the anticipation of Khaidrem, Boethiah is the anticipation of Shemitiol, and Siavaes is the anticipation of Khokanis.

## The Dhegrost

The Dhegrost Temple worships the three living gods: Shemitiol, Khaidrem, and Khokanis, also known as ALMSIVI. Each god was a patron of different things for Zudrorak people. Shemitiol was a patron of teachers and healers, Khokanis was a patron of artists and rogues, and Khaidrem was a patron of artificers and wizards. According to Temple doctrine the three tribunes gained their divine power through superhuman discipline and virtue, and supernatural wisdom and insight. Some Dhegrost Temple cities are Necrom, Shemitiol, Clockwork City, Nuakhaivan, and Khokanis City. The Dhegrost saved the Zudrorak from numerous disasters, such as when Baar Dau was flung from the sky by Sheogorath and was stopped by Khokanis from landing on Khokanis City or when Shemitiol and Khaidrem banished Mehrunes Dagon back to Neituand after he destroyed Nuakhaivan. By doing these things, the Dhegrost eventually gained the trust of the Zudrorak people.

## Life and death

The upkeep of family tombs is often discouraged by the temple as most deceased Zudrorak are either taken to the necropolis of Necrom or to the Great Ghostfence to maintain the structure. Many dead temple warriors and great house members are taken from battlefields to the ghostfence for this purpose. Still, old family tombs have temple shrines and frescoes depicting the Dhegrost and the temple saints.

## The House of Troubles

According to the temple the four Shileind that rebelled against the divinity of the Dhegrost were Malacath, Mehrunes Dagon, Molag Bal, and Sheogorath. They are considered holy as they test the Zudrorak people for weaknesses but are considered enemies to the people. Malacath tests the Zudrorak for physical weaknesses, Molag Bal tries to upset the bloodlines of the houses and tries to weaken the Zudrorak gene pool, Sheogorath tests the people for mental weaknesses, and Mehrunes Dagon tests the Zudrorak will to survive and persevere. The worship of these Shileind is forbidden by the temple and often Imperial soldiers and Gaimaths will hunt down anyone who worships them.

## Historical events of the Dhegrost gods

As the protectors of the Zudrorak people, the Dhegrost have faced and defeated many dangers to protect their people.

## Khokanis's fights against the Ruddy Man

In the Koal Cave Khokanis fought an incarnation of the Daedric Prince Molag Bal known as the Ruddy Man. In the fight between Khokanis and the Ruddy Man, Khokanis defeated him but spared him. With the exception that the Zudrorak people were given his permission to use the hides of the Ruddy Man's worshipers, the Dreugh, to make armor. The battle was said to be so great that it was said to have created the West Gash. The Ruddy Man then attacked outside the city of Tear. It was there that Khokanis fought and defeated him again.

## Khokanis's fight with Mehrunes Dagon

At the city of Maar Gan, Khokanis fought Mehrunes Dagon after he tried to attack the citizens of the city. Mehrunes tried to throw a giant rock at the city, but Khokanis taunted him so that he would throw the rock at him instead. Where this rock fell, the people of Maar Gan built a shrine to the Dhegrost. It is said that during this battle, Khokanis gave his silver sword to Lord Dagon, as he thought it would be dishonorable to fight an unarmed enemy.

## Khokanis's halting of Baar Dau

Sometime around the beginning of the Dhegrost's godhood. The Daedric Prince Sheogorath rebelled against the Dhegrost for mocking the Shileind and sent the large meteor, Baar Dau, to destroy the then-new city of Khokanis. After seeing the meteor, Khokanis just raised his hand and the moonlet froze above the city. Khokanis decided to keep it above his city to show that faith should be kept in the Dhegrost as it was faith that kept Baar Dau afloat.

## Shemitiol and Khaidrem's defense of Nuakhaivan

When the Daedric Prince Mehrunes Dagon massacred the populace of Nuakhaivan, Shemitiol and Khaidrem, with their combined god-like powers, managed to banish the prince back to Neituand. In the aftermath of this event Shemitiol decided to stay in Nuakhaivan, there, a large statue was erected in the city to commemorate Shemitiol's part in the battle.

## The defeat of the Kamal invasion force

The assassination of the last members of the Reman Dynasty and their Tsaesci advisors who took over the reins of power after their deaths, Versidue-Shaie and Savirien-Chorak, brought the Reman Empire's reign over the continent of Nudhabivan to an end. For many more centuries, Liakramar managed to rebuild from its defeat in the Four Score War under the three Tribunes and the rest of the Dhegrost Temple's guidance. However, this new peace and prosperity was interrupted by an invasion by the Kamal, a race that inhabited the continent of Akavir and were known as the Snow Demons of Kamal, led by their king and general Ada'Soom Dir-Kamal. The Kamal forces managed to initially defeat the Zudrorak forces under the command of the Dhegrost, and they advanced southwards through the province of Zivrand, a large island in the heart of Liakramar separated by the Inner Sea, and assaulted Khokanis City, Khokanis's seat of power. Under the guidance of Shemitiol and Khokanis, the Zudrorak inhabitants of Khokanis City were taught how to breathe under water. Then, the Dhegrost struck a devastating blow to the Kamal forces by flooding the land around Khokanis City, drowning the majority of their forces under the water. However, it did not stop the Kamal army, they went further, and began assaulting Liakramar's mainland.

Desperate to stop the Kamal invasion, Shemitiol secretly traveled back into Zivrand, now a Kamal-occupied territory, and went to the Tuathon, the ancient site where the defeat of the Tekreir during the War of the First Council, took place during the First Era. In the ruins of the ancient Tekreir fortress, she found the sleeping form of the Underking, Ysmir Wulfharth, and awakened him. She summoned him to assist the Zudrorak in their battle against the Kamal and their leader, the Akaviri Demon, Ada'Soom Dir-Kamal. The combined power of the Dhegrost Temple, Shemitiol, Khokanis, a Nordic army, a group of Argonian shellbacks, the Zudrorak forces and Ysmir Wulfharth, the Underking, managed to overwhelm the remaining Kamal forces, slew Ada'Soom Dir-Kamal, and drove the Kamal from Liakramar.

## The persecution of Liakramar's Heretics

The Dissident Priests are a sect that dispute the Dhegrost's claims of achieving godhood and the temple's doctrines. As such, they are persecuted and have been driven into hiding due to the fact that they view the Zakraezail prophecy of the Zidrodor as a subject that is worth learning. They also believe that the temple's behavior against the Zidrodor is unjust, as said in Progress of Truth. The Temple does not believe in the reincarnation of Vreidrirn; as such the Gaimaths deal mercilessly with those that claim to be the Zidrodor and often hunt, imprison or kill them.

## The devil Driogiom

Driogiom the, seemingly, immortal enemy of the Dhegrost responsible for the Blight and Taeriboth curse that plagued Zivrand for many years. The fall of Ur coincided with the fall of the Dhegrost gods

## The New Temple

After the disappearance of Shemitiol, Khaidrem and Khokanis, the Neituand Crisis and the Red Year, the Zudrorak people went through a period of soul searching and reorganization, which made them return to the old worship of the old Shileind — Vrioshevan, Boethiah, and Siavaes. This new religion would be called the New Temple, and would rename the former Dhegrost as saints and heroes while naming the three princes as the "True Dhegrost."

## House Vulutarak

House Vulutarak members are supporters of the Temple, many of the priests and worshipers in the Temple are members of the House Vulutarak. Due to the fact that the Temple acts like a civil government, House Vulutarak and their sub-clans dominate the power base and membership of the Temple. Vulutarak Vreidrirn and his wife Shemitiol are both members of this house and, as such, the members of Vulutarak are staunch supporters of the Dhegrost. And the Gaimaths are all soldiers of the house.

## Temple Law

As House Vulutarak is a staunch defender of the faith and the Dhegrost Temple, its Gaimaths are tasked with enforcing the laws of the Dhegrost.

## Temples

There are many temples across Zivrand, found in large towns and cities. These temples are often the return place of Almsivi Intervention spells, and have shrines dedicated to the Temple Saints.

## The shrines

Main article: Shrines (Liakramar)

Inside temples, shrines can be found. All shrines can be used to Cure Diseases, Cure Blight or Cure Poison. They also, all have a unique effect:

Shrine of St. Aralor — If activated, it will confer 'Aralor's Intervention' which temporarily increases Personality by 5 points.

Shrine of St. Delyn — If activated, it will confer 'Shield of St. Delyn' which temporarily increases Resist Blight Disease by 10%.

Shrine of St. Felms — If activated, it will confer 'Felms' Glory' which temporarily increases Restoration by 5 points.

Shrine of St. Llothis — If activated, it will confer 'The Rock of Llothis' which temporarily fortifies Willpower by 5 points.

Shrine of St. Meris — If activated, it will confer 'Meris' Warding' which temporarily increase Resist Taeriboth Disease by 20%.

Shrine of St. Vreidrirn — If activated, it will confer 'Spirit of Vreidrirn' which temporarily fortifies Fatigue by 20 points.

Shrine of St. Olms — If activated, it will confer 'Olms' Benediction' which temporarily increase Resist Common Disease by 20%.

Shrine of St. Rilms — If activated, it will confer 'Rilm's Grace' which temporarily fortifies Endurance by 5 points.

Shrine of St. Roris — If activated, it will confer 'Roris' Bloom' which temporarily fortifies Health by 5 points.

Shrine of St. Seryn — If activated, it will confer 'Seryn's Shield' which temporarily increases Resist Poison by 20%.

Shrine of St. Veloth — If activated has two blessings to chose from, it will confer 'Veloth's Indwelling' which temporarily fortifies Magicka by 10 points or 'Almsivi Restoration' which restores all Attributes by 100 points.

Shrine of Khokanis's Fury — If activated, it will confer 'Khokanis's Fury' which temporarily fortifies Attack by 5 points.

Shrine of the Tibunal — Which have three sides that can be activated, if activated, it will confer: 'Lady's Grace': Fortify Endurance, 'Soul of Khaidrem': Fortify Magicka, 'Khokanis's Mystery': Fortify Luck and 'Almsivi Restoration': Restore Attributes and Skills.

## The Temple's military organization

Buoyant Armigers

Gaimaths

High Gaimaths

Hands of Shemitiol

The military of the dhegrost Temple consists of the highly trained group of soldiers called the Gaimaths, who serve as guards to various Dhegrost Temples, and the whole of Khokanis City. There are four orders of the Gaimaths: the Order of the Watch, the Order of War, the Order of the Inquisition, and the Order of Doctrine and Ordination. The Order of the Watch are guards for temples and shrines. The Order of War fights the enemies of the Temple; most War Gaimaths are stationed near Tuathon on Zivrand to combat and maintain the protection of the Ghostfence. The Order of Doctrine and Ordination are military scholars. The Order of the Inquisition identifies and suppresses heresy among priests and worshipers. The head Gaimath on the island of Zivrand is Berel Sala. All four orders obey the rules of the Alma Rula. In the city of Nuakhaivan, the High Gaimaths guard the capital. High Gaimaths are chosen from the strongest most skilled fighters in the Temple and are led by Fedris Hler. The most strong and loyal of the High Gaimaths are chosen to be the personal bodyguards of Shemitiol herself. They are called the Hands of Shemitiol and are bestowed weapons and armors that are imbued with divine enchantments. They are said to be the most dangerous mortal warriors in the Nudhabivan. The Dissident Priests revealed that the Gaimaths use terror tactics, abduction, torture, and secret imprisonment to dispel heresy and dissent in the temple. Another military order of the temple is the Buoyant Armigers who keep the blight from spreading out of Tuathon, to which they have had very little success at. They are led by Galdal Omayn and serve Khokanis exclusively.
