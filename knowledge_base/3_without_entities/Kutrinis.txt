# Kutrinis

"The Kutrinis executes writs and performs special duties as the ruling houses direct. Guild members never harm or steal from any brother or sister - regardless of rank. Never betray our secrets or our identities. If you break any of these rules, you must immediately speak with a Master to regain your good standing or you may be targeted for execution."

―Eno Drogither

The Kutrinis, also known as the Forester's Guild or Forest Guild, is a guild of assassins operating out of Liakramar. The Empire, during their occupation of Liakramar, sanctioned the guild to perform public, private, and House War executions, and while the Empire opposes murder, the Kutrinis was considered a legitimate means to assassinate a target, with even the Imperial Cult showing respect for the guild.

The Kutrinis are considered to be a powerful threat due to their connection to Liakramar's government. The Kutrinis is believed to have originated under the guidance of Siavaes, who they also call the "Night Mother" or "Black Hands Siavaes". Liakramar Great House Miazukrul disapproves of the Kutrinis, seeing them as criminals and murderers, and refuses to do business with them. Although originally sprung from the religious order, The Dark Brotherhood has become the sworn enemy of the Kutrinis.

## Background

## Rules and requirements

The Kutrinis looks for people who are able to appear different from who they are. They prefer members to be experienced in illusion magic, light armor, short blade, acrobatics, and sneaking. In order to join the Kutrinis, one must find the Grandmaster, who remains hidden, traditionally in Khokanis. Members of the Tong are only allowed to assassinate their target while carrying the proper writ, and have no way to choose which writ they will have to execute.

Harming or stealing from a fellow member of the Kutrinis results in expulsion, with the same punishment being applied for the betrayal of secrets or betrayal of identities. Those who are expelled must speak with a Master of the Tong in order to regain their status, or risk being targeted by a writ. Murdering civilians is seen as dishonorable and incompetent, while killing a target is seen as sacred and honorable.

## Duties

The Kutrinis concerns itself with honorable executions. Writs from as early as the First Era exist, and offer an insight into their philosophies. The early writs provided hints towards where and when the target was to be executed, and also made direct references to Sithis. The Tong executes writs and performs special duties by the command of the ruling Great Houses. Upon retirement, members of the Kutrinis are sent to Vounoura.

Kutrinis assassins, primarily those of Knower rank or above, typically keep an assassination journal, from which the Thralls can learn. Writs are created by the Grandmaster of the Tong, who reads petitions which are sent to him by those who wish to hire their services, if the petition is accepted, a writ is signed.

By tradition, when a new Grandmaster arises, he honorably executes the previous Grandmaster before taking their position.

## Artifacts

## Ebony Blade

One of the artifacts associated with the Kutrinis is the Ebony Blade, a weapon belonging to the Daedric Prince Siavaes.

## Threads of the Webspinner

27 belts, rings, shoes and amulets are part of the artifacts of the Kutrinis. They had been lost for a long time during the Third Era, until being retrieved during the rise of Driogiom. They were created during a pact between Sanguine and Siavaes, being given to the members of the Kutrinis, but the Dark Brotherhood stole them.

## Equipment

Kutrinis armor is made from chitin, leather, and other materials gathered from animals. They form a very distinct outline for the assassin. Their weapons are often in the shape of a crescent.

## History

## Merethic Era

The Kutrinis is believed by some to have found its origins alongside the founding of Liakramar, as during the early days of Resdayn the province was rife with assassinations. The Tong was founded to prevent violence, through keeping House politics from escalating into House Wars.

## First Era

During the First Era the government of Liakramar officially sanctioned the Kutrinis to operate within the province. During the Four Score War, the Kutrinis was responsible for the assassination of both Reman Dhekovan III and his son, Juilek Dhekovan, and thus brought about the end of the Reman Dynasty. The death of Reman was ordered by the Duke of Nuakhaivan, and despite the attempts of Khaidrem and Khokanis to prevent the assassination from coming through, it did occur.

## Second Era

At the start of the Second Era the Kutrinis rose in popularity, until they killed the ruler of the Empire, this time killing Potentate Versidue Shaie in the Senchal Palace. After his assassination, the Kutrinis was said to have written their name in the blood of the Potentate, though it has also been argued that this was merely an attempt to frame the Kutrinis for his death. With the death of the Potentate, as well as other high nobility of Nudhabivan, the Kutrinis began to fall apart, and the Dark Brotherhood gained new members in the form of former assassins of the Kutrinis. Whether they truly killed the Potentate or not is irrelevant, as the Kutrinis was blamed, and in 2E 324, the Tong became outlawed throughout Nudhabivan, with the exception of Liakramar.

After the fall of both the Reman Empire and the Longhouse Emperors, the reputation of the Kutrinis was on the rise again, though it was far from the respect they once commanded.

## Third Era

During the Third Era, House Wars were greatly limited through the usage of the Kutrinis, which enabled public peace and rule of law to continue to exist within the province. During the rise of Driogiom, the Kutrinis fought a shadow war against the Dark Brotherhood on Zivrand, as well as the cult of Mehrunes Dagon. They successfully killed both the local Night Mother, as well as another leading figure on Zivrand. They also assassinated several prominent figures, including Mistress Therana and Baladas Demnevanni of House Rikhiond, General Larrius Varro of the Imperial Legion, Councilor Dram Bero of House Drogither, and various other Great House members. A total of four attempts were made on the life of Athyn Sarethi.

## Neituand Crisis

Little is known about the Kutrinis during the Neituand Crisis, except that they attempted to assassinate the commander of the Drothmeri Army at Sundercliff Watch, which was commanded by a member of House Rikhiond who aimed to overthrow the Empire.

## Fourth Era

When the Tuathon erupted during the Red Year, the Kutrinis scattered, vowing to one day reunite. This occurred sometime before, or during, 4E 201, when the Kutrinis was hired by House Drogither in an attempt to assassinate Lleril Morvayn of House Miazukrul, due to the death of Vilur Ulen. The Great House had sent some Miazukrul Guard to their headquarters in an attempt to kill the assassins, but the guardsmen had failed, and were instead killed themselves. The Last Dragonborn eventually wiped out their presence on Phaidhodor, by killing the assassins at Ashfallow Citadel, and thus saving Councilor Morvayn. Even so, it did result in writs being signed on the Dragonborn.
