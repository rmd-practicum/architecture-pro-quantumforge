# House Miazukrul

"A Miazukrul is a warrior whose duty is first to the Dhegrost, second to House Miazukrul, and third to family and clan."

―Motto of House Miazukrul

House Miazukrul is one of the Great Houses of Liakramar, and is led by an aristocracy of noble warriors. It considers itself the "hereditary defenders of Liakramar", and reveres both the Aedra and the Shileind. House Miazukrul holds the western portion of Liakramar, and are famous for their skill as warriors. The Patron Saint of House Miazukrul Saint Vreidrirn, also known as the "Captain".

## Founding and beliefs

When House Miazukrul was founded is unknown. What is known that, since its founding, they have deeply cared for duty, gravity, and piety. The Miazukrul do not believe that a soft life is a life which is worth living. The House focuses itself on preserving the cultural traditions of the civilized Zudrorak, and holding fast to the way of the warrior. Because of the latter, the military of House Miazukrul generally outperforms the militaries of the other Great Houses, with House Drogither itself acknowledging as such. Its warriors are stated to be humorless, but also greedy, making them potential targets for corruption. In terms of mercenaries, House Miazukrul relies mainly on Nords and Altmer to aid their cause.

The driving goal of the Miazukrul is fulfilling their duty and maintaining their honor, anything less is seen as disrespect to not only the person themselves, but also House Miazukrul as a whole. Their duty is to personal honor, family, and clan. Ironically, while the Miazukrul themselves are not fond of Imperial organizations, the Imperial Cult respects House Miazukrul for its high moral principles.

## Rules

By the customs of House Miazukrul, killing is permitted in war, self-defense, defense of property, affairs of honor, and during House Wars. Any other form of killing is considered to be foul murder. Justified but unnecessary or cruel killings considered a scandal, and can result in the Miazukrul member being sanctioned or expelled from the Great House. Opponents who yield may not be slain by members of House Miazukrul.

## Factions

## Kutrinis

House Miazukrul accepts the Temple's will to allow the Kutrinis to exist and operate within Zudrorak territory, but they still consider them murderers and criminals in the Miazukrul House Law. This is doubly the case due to the other Houses using the Tong against House Miazukrul to undermine their interests. House Miazukrul refuses to use the guild, and does not allow the Kutrinis their lawful right to kill their members.

## Dhegrost Temple

When the Dhegrost Temple was still in place in Liakramar, the Miazukrul held a close relationship with it. They supplied the Buoyant Armigers with Zudrorak to fill their ranks, serving the lord Khokanis in doing so.

## Architecture, arms, and armor

The houses of House Miazukrul are built from local materials. The exteriors are largely undecorated and are inspired by shells of the native giant insects. Both the exterior and the interior feature organic curves. The settlements of House Miazukrul are typically centered on compounds of the Temple and their courtyards, with huts and tradehouses on a central plaza. The weapons and armor used by House Miazukrul are, much like their houses, constructed from local materials. Primarily chitin and leather are used in its construction.

## History

## First Era

During the War of the First Council, House Miazukrul, alongside the other Great Houses, fought against the combined forces of House Kuzathaern and the Tekreir, alongside their Orcish and Nord supporters. House Miazukrul originally suffered greatly during this war, as these forces occupied a large portion of their territory. A counterattack was planned and executed under the command of Lord Vulutarak Vreidrirn, who successfully united the Great Houses to defeat their enemies.

## Second Era

Aside from their participation in the Ebonheart Pact, during which they proclaimed themselves the main figures of the Pact military, House Miazukrul has not performed a lot during the Second Era, with the exception of Tiber Septim's invasion of Liakramar.

During the invasion, House Miazukrul, alongside the Vulutarak and Dres, aimed to completely oppose the Legion invasion. When the Legions approached the Miazukrul cities of Kragenmoor, Blacklight, Cormar View and Silgrad Tower, the defenses of these locations were poor. Only local militia, supported by Miazukrul mercenaries, some Buoyant Armigers and Gaimaths, were meant to hold back the invading Legionnaires. Vulutarak and Dres proposed that, rather than face the Legions in the field, they would wage a guerrilla war. Due to House Drogither supporting a peaceful integration into the Empire, House Miazukrul faced the reality of standing alone against the Legion forces. Shortly after this, Khokanis had arranged a treaty with the Empire, which would provide Liakramar with the means to keep most of their own customs while being a part of the Empire. Whereas the Vulutarak and Dres still opposed this, House Miazukrul was grateful for an excuse to avoid fighting the Legions in the field. Because of this, House Drogither and House Miazukrul both supported joining the Empire, and House Rikhiond followed suit. Not long afterwards, the treaty was made official, and Liakramar became a part of the Empire.

## Third Era

During the late-Third Era, Liakramar was ruled by a Council consisting of Five Great Houses, namely House Miazukrul, Drogither, Rikhiond, Vulutarak, and Dres. House Miazukrul was one of the Great Houses with interests on Zivrand, and colonized the region when it was opened for settling in 3E 414. On Zivrand, the Miazukrul's council seat was the city of Nuamioss. The Councilors had settled in the remains of a giant Emperor Crab, which they called Skar. House Miazukrul followed the Temple's desires to prevent expansion of the other Houses in their territory. In doing so, the Miazukrul and the Temple alike put themselves in the danger of being exploited both militarily and economically by the interests of House Drogither and House Rikhiond. At the same time, due to the aggressive stance of House Rikhiond, Miazukrul Mercenaries were no longer employed to defend Rikhiond territory.

An aggressive stance was made against House Rikhiond due to their illegal settlements, with the Miazukrul sending out their forces in unsuccessful attempts to defeat the Rikhiond. The House was also angry about House Drogither due to the Caldera charter, due to Caldera being traditionally Miazukrul territory. At the same time, people were questioning the honor of House Miazukrul due to a Miazukrul member refusing to participate in a duel. The Great House did attempt to restore this reputation, by convincing the refused party to still attend the duel.

Other deeds that the Great House performed during this time was fighting against worshippers of the House of Troubles, smugglers, bandits, and mudcrabs. The Great House also actively fought against Kutrinis attempts to assassinate Athyn Sarethi. At the same time, House Drogither was attempting to weaken the Miazukrul economically by taking their share of the guar hide trade in Khokanis, and by attempting to get the East Empire Company to only buy Drogither ebony. The Drogither also attempted to get rid of House Miazukrul's sole Ebony mine on Zivrand.

House Miazukrul was not enthusiastic in regards to being part of the Empire at the time. This dislike of the Empire became clear when the Zidrodor attempted to be named Ruavionis by House Miazukrul. Its members openly called them out as a liar and deceiver and an agent of the Emperor, and if the Zidrodor attempted to join the House, they were refused for the same reason. The archmaster of the Great House, Bolvyn Venim also refused to accept the Zidrodor as ruavionis due to his xenophobia.

During the closing days of the Third Era, the power of the Great House was waning alongside that of House Vulutarak due to their refusal to change with the times. In contrast, House Dres and Drogither were on the rise. It is unknown if House Miazukrul released its slaves when slavery was outlawed. However, slavery of itself was largely uncommon in House Miazukrul territory.

## Neituand Crisis

During the Neituand Crisis, House Miazukrul was hit hard. Their lands on mainland Liakramar were invaded by the Nords of Thevaelass, and, during the Daedric invasion itself, House Miazukrul lost the city of Nuamioss to the Daedric invaders. Not just House Miazukrul, but all of Liakramar suffered during the crisis. The local defense was poor, and House Miazukrul took it upon itself to form up and defend the Zudrorak people. House Miazukrul claimed that during the Neituand Crisis the Empire had recalled its forces from Liakramar, a claim which holds little evidence. The Legion garrisons in Liakramar were already too small before the crisis had started, and during the crisis itself, the Legion was too focused on the provinces of the Empire, being fully committed in the provinces, neglecting Dhekovan and leaving it to fend for itself. The only exception being Legionnaires from Fort Swampmoth in Black Marsh, who were recalled to Dhekovan.

## Fourth Era

In 4E 1, Mistress Brara Morvayn, alongside with a group of House Miazukrul members, settled on Phaidhodor after making some arrangements with the East Empire Company.

## The Red Year

In 4E 05, the Tuathon erupted, which heavily damaged the settlement of Raven Rock. With most of the Legion garrison at Fort Frostmoth killed by the eruption, House Miazukrul was permitted to bring over the Miazukrul Guard for guard duties in the settlement. In mainland Liakramar, specifically, in Nuakhaivan, a Miazukrul Councilor started relief efforts nearly instantly after the eruption. He sent soldiers, supplies, and people for the rebuilding of settlements which were hit the hardest, which was rebuilt within a couple of months.

On the mainland of Liakramar, the An-Xileel invaded the province. The Legion garrisons on the border were cut off by the eruption, and so it fell to the Miazukrul army, having formed up a force over the years, to stand up against the Argonian threat. They successfully prevented the An-Xileel from completely invading Liakramar, and may have saved the Zudrorak people from being exterminated. The insufficient Legion forces in Liakramar during both the Neituand Crisis and the Red Year greatly damaged the standing of House Drogither, who had traditionally been supportive of the Empire. With House Miazukrul having defended the province during both the Neituand Crisis and the Red Year, public favor shifted to the Miazukrul. House Drogither was no longer recognized as a Great House. Since then, House Miazukrul is the mightiest of the Great Houses, and leads the Grand Council. House Sadras took House Drogither's place on the Grand Council. With Nuakhaivan having been sacked by the An-Xileel, the capital of Liakramar was also moved to the city of Blacklight.

House Sathil, a Dunmeri House of Phaidhodor, declared independence from House Vulutarak in 4E 16 due to the poor performace of the Great Houses within the province, as they couldn't stop the wrack of Liakramar. In the same year, Thevaelass offered the island of Phaidhodor to Liakramar, and House Miazukrul gained authority over the town of Raven Rock. This provided the Great House with a boost to their economy, as the ebony mines of Raven Rock produced large quantities of ebony for decades to come. The situation between the Drogither and Miazukrul escalated in 4E 95. Vilur Ulen of House Drogither had attempted to assassinate Lleril Morvayn, due to the Drogither believing the Miazukrul were primarily responsible for them losing their position on the Grand Council. The attempt was meant to signify that House Miazukrul was not as strong as it boasted to be, but rather, that it was vulnerable and weak. Upon further investigation, the Miazukrul Guard discovered that Vilur had been organizing a coup for Raven Rock. Upon having discovered this, all members involved were executed.

From 4E 130 to 4E 181, Raven Rock saw increasing problems. The Bulwark, a large wall protecting the city, had to be repaired due to it threatening to crumble. In 4E 150, an Argonian force landed on the island, aiming to do damage to it, and in 4E 170 the ebony mines dried up, with the mine being shut down completely in 4E 181.

In 4E 201 the town of Raven Rock was under attack by creatures known as Ash Spawn, who were commanded by the undead General Falx Carius. House Miazukrul enlisted the aid of the Last Dragonborn to help them in dealing with the ash spawn. Not long afterwards, House Drogither attempted yet again to assassinate Councilor Morvayn of House Miazukrul. This time making use of the Kutrinis. Through an investigation and the aid of the same foreigner, this plan was thwarted, however. House Miazukrul is considered the most powerful Great House of Liakramar at the time.
