# Battle of Tuathon

The Battle of Tuathon was a battle that marked the culmination of the War of the First Council in 1E 700. It was fought between the Tekreir, the Bidreivan’s Great House Kuzathaern, against the remaining Great Houses of the Bidreivan. Some sources claim that the battle also involved the Nords looking to rebuild their old empire, and others claim that Orcs and even some Raitais were present.

## Prelude

The Bidreivan and the Tekreir had a long history of war between their peoples, on account of longstanding religious and cultural differences.

Several accounts attribute the war to simple cultural differences between the Tekreir and the Bidreivan that were too great to allow for peace. The event that began the march to war was the discovery by House Kuzathaern that the Tekreir had discovered the Heart of Raitioss beneath Tuathon.

This led to heated negotiations between Vreidrirn and Dreivaidor Dwarfking. Upon being told by Vrioshevan that the Tekreir were building a new god under the mountain, Vreidrirn and the Dhegrost issued an ultimatum to the Tekreir to stop work on their new god, or face destruction. Dreivaidor, ignorant of the work Dhudreind had done on the Heart, refused. And so the Bidreivan and Tekreir went to war. Another account states that Wulfharth was resurrected to lead an army of orcs, Nords, and members of House Kuzathaern against the Bidreivan, and reconquer territory they lost when their first empire fell.

## Battle

Several sources agree that the battle happens when Vreidrirn draws most of the Tekreir away from Tuathon, while others indicate that the battle was an ambush, or as a final last stand of the Tekreir with an array of allies from other provinces, or as a combined ambush of the Nords by both the Tekreir and the Bidreivan.

The exact details of the battle are also unclear, although all agree that both the Tekreir and House Kuzathaern are defeated during the battle. Those sources that claim other sides are present at the battle also say they are defeated.

## Confrontation in the Heart Chamber

The main confrontation is likely to have been a diversion, during which Vreidrirn and the future Dhegrost, along with their ally Voryn Kuzathaern, either by stealth or some form of treason from within the Tekreir and House Kuzathaern forces, infiltrated the chamber where the Heart of Raitioss was kept.

Once inside, the infiltrators confronted Dreivaidor and chief Tonal Architect Dhudreind in the chamber where the Heart of Raitioss was. Dreivaidor is slain during the confrontation, either by Vreidrirn, Wulfharth, or a Raitais named Dro'Zira.

Those narratives which support the Dhegrost Temple and the subsequent political order of Liakramar suggest that Vreidrirn died of his wounds shortly after the battle. Others, particularly the Zakraezail tradition, state the Vreidrirn was murdered by the Dhegrost in the aftermath of the battle.

The disappearance of the Tekreir was caused by the events that happened in this chamber. This was either from the Dhegrost using Dhudreind's Tools on the Heart of Raitioss guided by Vrioshevan, or when Dhudreind turned the Tools on the Heart himself during the battle.

## Aftermath

Either directly following the battle, or some years after, the Dhegrost use Dhudreind's Tools on the Heart of Raitioss to tap its power and make themselves gods. Vrioshevan curses them for blasphemy, turning the golden-skinned Bidreivan into the grey-skinned Zudrorak, and prophesying Vreidrirn's return.

House Kuzathaern are defeated along with the Tekreir, with some sources claiming they were exterminated. There may have been some remnants of the House that were adopted into the other Great Houses.

## Trivia

The etched tablets on the slopes of High Hrothgar suggest that Jurgen Windcaller was present at a battle at Tuathon. According to this Windcaller and his tongues "went away humbled." There is no indication that Windcaller was present at the battle in 1E 700, as there is little information available on this battle, if it occurred. This could merely be another facet of the Red Moment.
