# Nuamioss

"Nuamioss town is a large settlement in the Miazukrul village style, built of local materials, with organic curves and undecorated exteriors inspired by the landscape and by the shells of giant native insects."

―Anonymous

Nuamioss (Dunmeris: Old Home), sometimes spelled Ald-ruhn or referred to as Old Run, is one of the four major cities of the Zivrand district of the province of Liakramar. Nuamioss is a unique settlement in the Dark Elven province, built from the foundation of the carcass of Skar, the Emperor Crab of legend. The crab was defeated by the combined forces of House Hleran and the Zakraezails.

## Geography

The original Zakraezail camp called Nuamioss was vast, occupying the hills in between the Ashlands of Tuathon and the West Gash. The area around Skar served as the meeting grounds, with a large campfire in the center of the camp. West from the campsite were fewer yurts and open areas for the herders of Zivrand. Dirt paths surrounded the main campsite which lead into shrines of the four Great Zakraezail Tribes of the sub-continent. A road south of Nuamioss leads into the Daedric ruins of Ramimilk. Several landmarks surround the camp including the Foyada Drura and the Hleran Ancestral Tomb. As time went on, the Dark Elves of House Miazukrul had begun to inhabit the area, establishing the Great City of Nuamioss. Skar was refurbished as the Manor District of the town, and the yurts were replaced with the traditional Miazukrul homes. The new residents of Nuamioss created walls surrounding the city, protecting the city from the ash storms of the region.

The former campsite was remade as the town square of the Nuamioss city, and the rest of the city revolves around this area. The road leading into the Nuamioss Manor District has several artisan services ranging from the libraries or the blacksmiths of House Miazukrul. The entrance of Nuamioss has twin watchtowers overlooking the area, and it leads to the southern town square, with the local Mages Guild chapter and the Rat in the Pot Tavern occupying the area. Northwestern Nuamioss is the home to several societal businesses including the Ald Skar Inn and the Council Club for House Miazukrul in Zivrand. Eastern Nuamioss has various residential buildings lacking walls to protect it. The center of the area is the Nuamioss Temple, the local place of worship for the Dhegrost Temple. North of the temple is a private estate to the nobles of House Miazukrul.

## History

## First Era

## The Battle against Skar; the Zakraezail's Nuamioss

The sub-continent of Zivrand and overall Liakramar has housed all sorts of alien creatures, varying in sizes. The bugs that House Miazukrul have used no longer exists and so on. A large species of crab called the Emperor Crab had lived on Zivrand during the time of the Bidreivan, and the most dangerous one was Skar. Skar terrorized the residents of Zivrand, which was mainly the Zakraezails. It took the combined forces of all the Zakraezails and a champion of House Hleran, Dranoth Hleran, to take out the large beast. Dranoth had utilized a powerful spear called the Calderas, which became a legend afterward. The carcass of Skar was refurbished as a meeting place for the Zakraezails of Zivrand, should anything affect their home, they can convene in the newly-established settlement named Nuamioss.

## Second Era

Main article: Nuamioss (Online)

## Ancestral Ties to Nuamioss; Enter Drelyth Hleran

During the Interregnum in 2E 583, the meeting grounds of Nuamioss was visited by Drelyth Hleran, a member of House Hleran whose ancestry had ties to the settlement. Drelyth attempted to commune with the Zakraezails inhabiting the camp, but they would not talk to him, believing he was untrustworthy. The Dark Elf had asked another adventurer known as the Vestige to help him negotiate with the Zakraezails and to uncover the truth. The Vestige spoke with Farseer Kuamta, who gave the warrior a blessing and directed them to the four shrines of the Zakraezail tribes. With the blessings, the Vestige talked with Wise Woman Asani and was shown the way to the Hleran Ancestral Tomb, where they communed with the spirit of Dranoth Hleran. After proving their pure intent, Dranoth bestowed the Vestige with the legendary spear named Calderas. The traveler than gave Drelyth the spear and with the weapon, the Zakraezails trusted the Hleran Elf, and other Dark Elves of Zivrand began to commune with the local Zakraezails. This event was the beginning of the Great City of Nuamioss.

## The False Zidrodor; Conoon Chodala of Urshilaku

Around the same time, Conoon Chodala was the Ashkhan of the Urshilaku Tribe in Zivrand. He claimed that he was, in fact, the Zidrodor, and in his hubris he allied with the Red Exiles, an exiled group of Zakraezails to harass the tribes of Zivrand, so that they can accept Chodala as the Zidrodor of Liakramar. Chodala's sister, Seryna attempted to convince Chodala to stop his wrath, but was ignored by her brother. Seryn work along with the Vestige to convince Chodala to end his masquerade, and so they traveled to the Cavern of the Incarnate, where they would commune with the Failed Incarnates, to create a convincing argument against Chodala. At a meeting in Nuamioss, the claims were put against Chodala but he dismissed them, attacking the Zakraezails in Nuamioss in retaliation. Chodala had been absorbing Khokanis's powers, causing much distress in Zivrand. It was revealed later that Chodala was under the influence of Barbas, who attempted to find the entrance to the Clockwork City. They were both stopped by the Vestige, Seryn and Barilzar.

## Third Era

Main article: Old Run (Arena)

## Old Run during the Imperial Simulacrum

During the Imperial Simulacrum from 3E 389 to 3E 399, Emperor Uriel Septim VII was imprisoned along with Talin Warhaft in the Deadlands by Jagar Tharn. Tharn planned for months to capture the throne, and he was able to achieve so in the span of ten years. Tharn had a powerful weapon called the Staff of Chaos, and he split it into nine pieces and scattered them across Nudhabivan, the staff was the only thing keeping Uriel Septim and Warhaft in Neituand. A warrior escaped his clutches and traveled all corners of Nudhabivan to retrieve the missing pieces. The hero was known as the Eternal Champion, and he assembled the staff and defeated Jagar Tharn at the Imperial Palace. The Eternal Champion had at one point visited the town of Old Run in their quest to recover the pieces.

## The True Zidrodor; Duel against Bolvyn Venim

Main article: Nuamioss (Liakramar)

During the Zivrand Crisis in 3E 427, the actual Zidrodor had revealed themselves and had begun their quest to fulfill the Zidrodor Prophecy. Parts of the prophecy must have the Zidrodor attempt the Great Houses of Zivrand and made their attempt to persuade the Councilors of House Miazukrul to name them the Ruavionis of the Great House Miazukrul. The warrior traveled to the city of Nuamioss, the House Miazukrul capital of Zivrand to speak with Bolvyn Venim, the Archmaster of House Miazukrul. Despite their proof, Venim refused to name the Zidrodor the Ruavionis and so the only way it can be dissolved was for the champion to duel Bolvyn Venim. And so, both warriors traveled to the city of Khokanis and dueled each other, with the Zidrodor claiming victory. After convincing the other Councilors, the warrior was named the Ruavionis of the Great House Miazukrul, being one step closer to fulfilling the prophecy.

## Remember Miazukrul; the Fall of Nuamioss

During the Neituand Crisis in 3E 433, the city-state of Nuamioss was besieged by the Daedric forces of Neituand. The Fall of Nuamioss is one of the most well-known battles of the conflict. The battle was in favor of the Shileind, House Miazukrul was in a losing fight, and so, the conjurers of Nuamioss had resurrected the great Emperor Crab, Skar. However, even the mighty crustacean of Skar was unable to defeat the invading force. The city of Nuamioss was lost, and the Daedric Armies advanced deep into the Ashlands to Ghostgate, attempting to break the gate. It is unknown whether the Daedric Forces broke the gate, but in the end, the Neituand Crisis was adverted when Martin Septim had sacrificed himself to defeat Mehrunes Dagon in the Imperial City.

## Fourth Era

## The Red Year & Raven Rock

In 4E 5, the province of Liakramar had undergone a very severe disaster where the Ministry of Truth had crashed onto the city of Khokanis and Tuathon had erupted, destroying the sub-continent of Zivrand and the settlements inhabiting the region. Nuamioss was destroyed amidst the conflict. Before this, after the Neituand Crisis, the Great Houses had sent small groups to spread throughout the province. One of the House Miazukrul groups was led by Brara Morvayn, who lived in Nuamioss and was a member of the Miazukrul Council. Morvayn had immediately traveled to the island of Phaidhodor and took refuge in the Imperial township of Raven Rock. After several negotiations, Raven Rock had integrated the Great House Miazukrul into their society, making the mining town flourish even more than before. Brara eventually became the leader of Raven Rock, and House Miazukrul had claimed full ownership of the town, after passing away from old age. Her son, Lleril Morvayn took up the position of the Councilor of Raven Rock. Meanwhile, in Nuakhaivan, a House Miazukrul had undergone a relief effort to rebuild the settlements of Zivrand including Nuamioss. It is unknown if it was completely rebuilt.
