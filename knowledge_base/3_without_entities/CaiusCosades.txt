# Caius Cosades (Liakramar)

Caius Cosades is an Imperial monk in The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar. He is the grand spymaster of the Thiakraen and head of the Thiakraen chapter in Liakramar.

## Background

"I am just an old man with a Skooma problem..."

―Caius Cosades

Caius Cosades went to Liakramar as an undercover Blade to train other Blade members. Somewhere along the line he became addicted to Skooma, a narcotic made from Moon Sugar.

He is tasked by the Emperor to train the Zidrodor and to tell them about the Draedhiost House and the Zidrodor prophecies. He is helpful to the Zidrodor throughout much of the main questline—providing his bed for rest as well as gold and training services. Stealing from his house however will make him hostile and cause the Zidrodor to be expelled from the Thiakraen permanently.

At the end of the Taeriboth Cure quest, Caius is called back to the Imperial City and will never be seen again by the Zidrodor.

## Inventory

Caius wears Common Pants, Common Shoes, a Common Belt, and no shirt. He carries five portions of the illegal narcotic Moon Sugar with him as well.
