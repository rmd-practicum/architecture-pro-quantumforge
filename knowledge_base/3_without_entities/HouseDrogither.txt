# House Drogither

"To trade fairly and freely is to honor the Three."

―Motto of House Drogither

House Drogither was one of the Great Houses of Liakramar. The patron Saint of House Drogither is Saint Veloth. The symbol of House Drogither is a scale, which represents balance, trade, and compromise. The House is led by traders and merchant princes, and prefers to use Imperials and Redguards as mercenaries. House Miazukrul considers the Drogither to be liars and untrustworthy.

## Territory

The capital of House Drogither is the city of Narsis. House Drogither territory focuses itself on central Liakramar. On Zivrand, the territory of House Drogither was primarily in the west.

## Philosophy

House Drogither considers itself the most open and progressive of the Dunmeri Great Houses, having openly embraced Imperial law and custom, which for a long time, gave them power. House Drogither's strong economy enables them to hold influence over the Miazukrul and the Temple, due to them requiring coin for wars or donations. House Dres, like the Drogither, knows how to make money, but does not know how to avoid making enemies. House Drogither cares about reputation like they care about opportunity, balancing the two in order to get the best outcome. House Drogither has experience with Kwama Egg mines, knowing its produce and how to make them profitable.

Due to House Drogither not having large armies or many holdings, they focus on diplomacy and negotiating. Their skill at talking also resulted in many becoming blackmailers and backstabbers. House Drogither always wants to live in peace with their neighbors, believing that peace is the only way to get sustainable growth. While many members are corrupt to some extent, the Drogither are not overly concerned about this, as they generally do not harm the House, and sometimes still serve the common good.

The Drogither aim to strive for balance in their dealings. When one side gets a favor too large, the other side becomes unpredictable, and can cause strife. In order to get a fair deal, all parties involved must be rid of their greed, wants, and strife. The weapon of House Drogither comes in the form of trade, and when supply and demand compliment each other, the Drogither are strong. As such, the House is willing to trade with anyone who would desire honest trade with the Drogither. The Drogither are an aggressive mercantile culture, influenced by Imperial values such as progress, tolerant polytheism, and free enterprise. Through their support of the Empire and its principles, House Drogither saw itself favored by many Imperial factions, such as the Imperial Cult.

## Arms and armor

For the House Drogither guards and marines, the Drogither make use of various materials. Armor generally makes use of guar hides, steel, or bonemold. Bows are made from imported ash or yew wood, quivers being made of guar leather.

## Architecture

Drogither architecture is divided into three categories. The public buildings, private dwellings, and plantations. Public buildings are multi-storied, rectangular, and feature arched entranceways and decorated exteriors. Private dwellings are similar, though with less decorations. Plantations are similar to Temple compounds, having the plantation itself walled around, with a grand manor taking the spot of where normally a Temple would be.

## History

## First Era

Like the other Great Houses, House Drogither was involved in the conflict with the Tekreir and the Draedhiost House, known as the War of the First Council. Under the command of Lord Vulutarak Vreidrirn, the Great Houses united and achieved victory over their enemies.

## Second Era

In 2E 559, House Drogither and House Dres waged a house war over a territorial dispute. House Drogither desired to expand its territory by creating a trading post near the border of Black Marsh. The Dres however, were determined to prevent this from coming true. During construction, Dres mercenaries engaged with the Drogither, and came close to coming out victorious, but Drogither reinforcements were called upon via a portal, resulting in victory.

## Ebonheart Pact

House Drogither openly supported the Ebonheart Pact during the Alliance War, openly embracing their ancient enemies as friends. This decision was due to them using the opportunity to gain power in Deshaan. Both Narsis and Nuakhaivan having been cities with great Drogither influence. Within the Gold Coast, House Drogither was in good relations with the Gold Coast Trading Company. The Company used this in order to sell items that had been stolen from the Imperial City after it had fallen.

## Llodos Plague

During the Alliance War, the Llodos Plague broke out in the Deshaan region of Liakramar. While at first the settlement of Serkamora was struck, the Maulborn, in command of the plague, chose to continue to Narsis. The Drogither capital of Narsis was struck by the plague, resulting in many of its residents fleeing to Nuakhaivan. The nearby lake of Lake Drogither was also monitored and secured by the maulborn. In order to provide aid to the sick in Narsis, House Drogither organized a caravan with supplies in Nuakhaivan, however, this caravan was ambushed by Dres mercenaries under the command of the Maulborn. The Maulborn were eventually defeated by the Vestige.

## Tiber War

During the Tiber War, House Drogither was the smallest and weakest of the Great Houses, barely holding their status in their ancient capital of Narsis. At the time, they were considered traditional enemies of the Great House Vulutarak, who had controlled Liakramar's government for some 3000 years. House Drogither had limited trade with the Empire at the time. When Tiber Septim's Imperial Legions invaded Liakramar, House Drogither was eager to propose accommodation. When eventually House Rikhiond and Miazukrul followed suit, the Lord High Chancellor of the Grand Council, an Vulutarak, refused to accept the treaty. As such, he was assassinated, and a Drogither took his place. The Drogither used this opportunity to deal with old conflicts they had with House Vulutarak, and a number of local councils changed hands in coups. Drogither influence further increased as many of House Vulutarak's nobility chose to commit suicide rather than submit to the Empire, as a result of which, House Drogither took most of their power.

## Third Era

From 3E 368 to 3E 389, Emperor Uriel Septim VII used the Drogither in Liakramar to support Imperial interests. Through House Drogither, Imperial culture spread into Liakramar, and the province started to develop its economy. Later on, Uriel would also use his power to draw power from the Great Houses to the court of King Drogither Helseth, in order to gain a stronger foothold in the province. He also took advantage of the weakening of the Dhegrost Temple to promote the Imperial Cult, which later succeeded in becoming the dominant faith in the districts of House Drogither and Zivrand.

From 3E 383 to 3E 433, many Zudrorak migrated to Cheydinhal in Dhekovan in order to escape persecution by the Dhegrost Temple within Liakramar. One of these Zudrorak was a Drogither named Andel Indarys, who migrated to Cheydinhal looking for opportunity. He succeeded, eventually becoming Count of the city.

In 3E 414 Zivrand was opened up for settlement by the King of Liakramar. House Drogither, alongside Imperial colonists, started expanding and developing parts of the land, taking hold over areas of Zivrand. They created the settlements of Pelagiad and Caldera. Due to their cooperation with the Empire, House Drogither had a great political and economical standing in Liakramar, openly welcoming Imperial law, the Legions, Imperial bureaucracy and freedom of trade and religion. The Drogither still honored the ancestors, Temple, and ways of the Great Houses, but also adapted to the progress in the rest of the Empire. In contrast to the other Houses, House Drogither did not dislike non-Zudrorak, and desired to live in prosperity with the other races, as well as contribute to the grownth of the Empire.

## Zivrand Crisis

Driogiom, in Tuathon, however, was planning to use his power to weaken both the Legions as well as the Drogither. In 3E 426 and 3E 427 the Draedhiost House started with assassinating prominent members of House Drogither and the Empire. Driogiom was eventually defeated by the Zidrodor. At the same time, local unrest in Drogither Districts was decreased due to collaboration with the Legion. House Drogither did not openly support the Zidrodor, but did not oppose him either, due to their virtue of freedom of religion, so long as public order was not threatened.

At the time, the Camonna Tong held a great deal of power over House Drogither. It was said that Orvas Dren and his Camonna Tong would try to push out the Empire the moment it showed signs of weakness, such as due to a recalling of the Legions. The influence of the Camonna Tong, specifically, Orvas Dren became apparent when the Zidrodor desired to be named Drogither Ruavionis. Due to Velanda Omani and Nevena Ules being under the service of Orvas Dren, neither would name the Zidrodor Ruavionis until Orvas Dren did. Dren himself, having had xenophobic visions of Driogiom, was hesitant to accept the Zidrodor as Ruavionis, and may have outright refused him. Orvas, despite being the brother of Duke Vedam Dren, did consider having him killed due to interference in Camonna Tong business. In contrast, House Drogither itself sought to damage the power that the Camonna Tong held over its policies, attempting to make Orvas give up his leading role in the Tong. At the same time, attempts were made to blackmail Orvas through helping one of his slaves escape. In contast to the Camonna Tong, the Drogither themselves were generally proud citizens of the Empire, and friends of Imperial factions in the land.

The Camonna Tong was not the only faction that the Drogither had a conflict with. House Miazukrul and the Drogither were at odds, due to the valuable ebony mines of Caldera, which the Drogither used to their advantage. The Drogither themselves did not own the charter to Caldera, which was instead owned by the Imperial Caldera Mining Company. The Drogither actively tried to gain an advantage over the Miazukrul, sending out spies to learn their orders, but also trying to cut off their guar hides from being sold in Khokanis City. The Drogither also considered shutting down the sole ebony mine held by the Miazukrul on Zivrand, due to the East Empire Company threatening to buy ebony from the Miazukrul due to the Drogither prices. House Drogither also attempted to convince the Zainab Zakraezails to solely sell their ebony to the Drogither. The House also watched for people who smuggled ebony.

The Drogither also had problems with the Rikhiond due to their illegal settlements outside of Rikhiond territory.

The corruption by the councilors of House Drogither was prevalent among some of its members. Yngling Half-Troll abused the faith of the Dhegrost Temple by using the funds meant for the restoration of the Drogither Temple for other affairs. Likewise, Drogither Councillor Velanda Omani attempted to gain land through a forged land deed. Odral Helvi, a House Drogither official working at Caldera, was also known for smuggling ebony from the Caldera ebony mines, though it is unknown if he was caught and trialed for this illegal act. Odral was not the only corrupt member of House Drogither in Caldera, however. Cunius Pelelius, a lawman of House Drogither, had gotten into some trouble in terms of coin. As a result of this, he started stealing ebony from the ebony mines in order to pay for his debts.

## Nuakhaivan

When Barenziah returned to Nuakhaivan with her son, Drogither Helseth, the previous Drogither King, known as Athyn Llethan suddenly died. With Helseth being crowned shortly afterwards, much discussion arose. One of his first acts was the outlawing of slavery within Liakramar, which resulted in a bloody conflict, with the Drogither and Dres both releasing their slaves and forming an alliance against the Vulutarak. His behavior resulted in many proclaiming Helseth to be a mere Imperial figurehead, however, some of his other actions did return more traditional ways. The Grand Council was reformed under Helseth's rule, and now included all the heads of the Great Houses. Within Nuakhaivan itself, some Drogither nobles disliked the notion of King Helseth being the ruler of Liakramar, and were attempting to overthrow him, though they ultimately failed in this attempt.

Under Helseth's rule, the power of the Great Houses was changing. House Dres and Drogither increased in their power due to embracing the changes. In contrast, Vulutarak and Miazukrul were on the decline, being unable to change with the times. Under the circumstances, Khokanis stated that House Drogither represented the future of Liakramar, integrated into the culture of the Empire.

## Fourth Era

## Decline of House Drogither

The aftermath of both the Neituand Crisis and the Red Year had a devastating effect on the position of House Drogither. During the Neituand Crisis, Liakramar's Legion garrisons were unable to properly defend Liakramar, which had been predicted by Duke Vedam Dren years before. Even so, they were fully committed in the province to fight the Shileind threat. Because of their low forces however, Liakramar was unable to properly defend itself against the Shileind horde until House Miazukrul took up arms and directed Liakramar's resistance against the Shileind. Five years later Tuathon erupted, which cut off what little remained of the Imperial garrisons at the border with Black Marsh. After the eruption, the Argonians under the command of the An-Xileel invaded Liakramar, and House Miazukrul was called upon to stop them. The Argonians cut through southern Liakramar, and even continued to occupy some of the border regions as late as 4E 201, but House Miazukrul managed to prevent them from cutting through the entire province.

Because of how poorly the Empire had been able to protect Liakramar, many Zudrorak took out their anger on the Drogither, due to them having the closest ties with the Empire. House Miazukrul claimed that the Empire had recalled its Legions to defend Dhekovan, which left Liakramar defenseless. Regardless of the truth of the matter, House Drogither was turned into the scapegoat of the suffering of the Zudrorak. They were no longer recognized as a Great House, and were removed from the Grand Council. Their seat on the Grand Council was now filled by the new House Sadras.

Over the years, House Drogither grew resentful of House Miazukrul, whom they held responsible for their fall from power. As such, they attempted both an assassination of the Miazukrul Councilor Lleril Morvayn and a coup on the settlement of Raven Rock on Phaidhodor in 4E 95, to show that the Miazukrul were not as powerful as they claimed to be. Both the assassination and the coup failed, however, being thwarted by the Miazukrul Guard stationed in the settlement.

Years later, in 4E 201, House Drogither would attempt another assassination on the councilor. Being led by Vendil Ulen, a descendant of the Vilur Ulen, who attempted the original coup and assassination in 4E 95. Vendil took on a fake last name, becoming known as Vendil Severin instead, and lived with a fake family of other members of House Drogither, those being Tilisu Severin and Mirri Severin. They arrived in Raven Rock in 4E 191, and were a great benefit to the settlement in the 10 years that they lived in the settlement, providing food, coin, supplies and even repairworks for the settlement. Through usage of Liakramar's legal assassins guild, known as the Kutrinis, they attempted to kill Councilor Morvayn. Their attempts were stopped by the Last Dragonborn, however, who met the assassins and the Ulen family at Ashfallow Citadel, defeating them. Because of this, Kutrinis assassins kept being sent out in attempts to get rid of the Dragonborn afterwards.
