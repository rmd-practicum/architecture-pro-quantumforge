# House Rikhiond

"The forceful expression of will gives true honor to the Ancestors."

―Motto of House Rikhiond

House Rikhiond is one of the Great Houses of Liakramar. The Rikhiond are a House of wizard-lords who have mastered the arcane arts, and are hostile to the Empire, Temple, and other Great Houses. Their capital is the city of Port Rikhionds in the northeast archipelago of the province. During the late Third Era, Khokanis described the Rikhiond as being similar to Khaidrem in that they are "iconoclastic, profane, [and] unconventional." The Rikhiond are the only known people with the power to raise life from ash, and are considered masters of enchanting in Liakramar. The patron saint of House Rikhiond is Saint Vorys. They are ruled by the Parliament of Bugs, a group of High Rikhiond wizards.

## Arms and armor

A lot of the Rikhiond arms and armor have some form of influence based on the fungi used by the Rikhiond. In the absence of metal, Rikhiond axes are even crafted from forge-hardened ferrofungus, and while generally not using Bows in the House, the House thralls of the Rikhiond are used as archers.

## Beliefs

The Rikhiond are known as being the most xenophobic of the Zudrorak, shunning contact with outsiders, and instead focusing themselves on their herds of giant insects in the rocky hills and islands of the northeast of Liakramar. The Rikhiond produce Rikhiond Bug Musk, a highly prized perfume among the Zudrorak, and the riding insects bred by the Rikhiond claim the highest prices on the markets of Nuakhaivan and Narsis. They strongly defend their right to own slaves, keeping many Argonians and Raitaiss as possession. Despite this, the Rikhiond will accept anyone, regardless of race or birthplace, to join their ranks, having to meet representatives known as "Mouths" of the Councilors of House Rikhiond. Some fear that the Rikhiond only accept people with high intelligence and willpower, but even this is exaggerated, as even modest folk are accepted.

Advancement in the House does depend on an increased intelligence and willpower, as well as proficiency in the magical arts. Even so, promotion remains difficult for outsiders, due to House Rikhiond openly accepting murder and assassination as legitimate means of advancement. The Rikhiond Wizard-Lords depend on loyal, well-paid, skilled retainers for services. Though recruitment of the Rikhiond does occur within the House itself, the House often needs to venture beyond their own to hire the required craftsmen and specialists.

House Rikhiond does not place constraint on the ambitions and enterprise of its members, according to ancient laws and customs of the Rikhiond. As such, the Rikhiond were permitted by their own House to settle on regions where they held no authority. The Rikhiond Council did openly acknowledge that these individuals were at the mercy of other factions in the region, but that the Rikhiond themselves would not get involved.

## Rules

The rules of the Rikhiond follow a pattern of might makes right. Any Rikhiond who successfully steals from another is permitted by House law to keep the item. Likewise, murdering an opponent by magic or treachery is also permitted, as it's the traditional way of settling disputes in the House. Members of House Rikhiond can still be expelled, however most members of the House will either not know, or care, about the expulsion.

## Factions

The following are the beliefs of House Drogither, Miazukrul, and the Imperial Cult on the Great House Rikhiond.

The practices of House Rikhiond were considered to be impious and inhumane by the members of the Imperial Cult, House Miazukrul considerings House Rikhiond to be a house of scheming, and while House Drogither recognizes the Rikhiond for their extensive knowledge of the arcane arts, they also point out how the House fights among itself more than others.

## Mushroom towers

Certain members of the Rikhiond live in elaborate mushroom towers, the reasoning as to why they live this way is kept from outsiders. What is known is that the Rikhiond grow the towers themselves through secret means, and that the Master Wizards of the House traditionally live in them.

Rikhiond members of the rank of Spellwright seeking further advancement in the House are required to construct a mushroom tower in order to advance to the rank of Wizard. The foundations of the mushroom towers are formed from the souls of two powerful Shileind, most commonly Storm Atronachs, Winged Twilights, or Golden Saints. These souls are stored in Grand Soul Gems, and placed at the base of the mushroom tower. The strong souls from these Shileind form the foundation of the tower, and help it to grow. Rikhiond towers commonly had some form of levitation magic required to gain full access to the tower.

Within House Rikhiond, advancing beyond the rank of Lawman requires a patron who a person can be the Mouth of. The Mouths of House Rikhiond represent their patrons on the Rikhiond Council and do chores for their master. Traditionally, the new Mouth is awarded a Silver Staff of Peace by their patron. Another traditonal reward by a patron for good service is the Silver Staff of War. Favored magical skills of the Rikhiond are Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Enchanting, Illusion and Mysticism.

While those of the rank of Wizard and above have the right to participate on the Council, members are not obligated to actually participate in the Rikhiond politics. Those who join the Council can also leave again at a later date if they so choose.

The highest achievable ranks of Master, Magister, and Archmagister are related to one another. The Archmagister is the head of House Rikhiond, and those wishing to replace them are advanced to the rank of Magister. In contrast, Wizards seeking advancement without a desire to lead the House, can instead be promoted to Master. Those who accept the advancement to Magister publicy announce their belief that they have the right to rule the House, and must defeat the ruling Archmagister to take their spot, while also needing the support of the other Councilors of the House.

## History

## First Era

During the First Era, House Rikhiond, like the other Great Houses, was involved in a conflict against the Draedhiost House and the Tekreir in Liakramar. The Tekreir and the Draedhiost House, alongside their Orc and Nord allies, were initially greatly successful, taking up most of the Miazukrul, Rikhiond, and Zivrand territories. The Great Houses suffered numerous defeats, until Lord Vulutarak Vreidrirn was appointed Ruavionis, and united the Houses to fight back, eventually finding victory.

One Rikhiond retainer is the author of the book "The Real Vreidrirn", detailing the creation of Resdayn, and a short history of the land up until the death of Vreidrirn. The book claims that the Moon-and-Star could only be worn by Vreidrirn himself, and that anyone else who tried to wear it would find death.

## Second Era

During the Second Akaviri Invasions, the Great House Rikhiond had their peninsula ignored by the Akaviri hordes, who landed in Thevaelass instead. Because their lands weren't threatened by the invasion, the Rikhiond didn't care about the forming of the Ebonheart Pact either, and chose not to join it, being friends of neither the Argonians or the Nords. When the Pact formed, the Rikhiond were said to have released every other House trooper they had in their employ.

## Tiber War

When Tiber Septim's Imperial Legions invaded Liakramar, House Rikhiond was neutral in how to respond to the threat. House Drogither proposed accommodation, whereas the Vulutarak, Dres, and Miazukrul proposed opposition. When House Miazukrul had to face the brunt of the Legion forces on its own, it was eager for an excuse to leave the opposition. When Khokanis announced a proposed treaty, House Miazukrul changed sides, now supporting accommodation instead. House Rikhiond, seeing the situation, decided to follow suit. Eventually, Liakramar joined the Empire as one of the last provinces, having officially joined it through treaty.

## Third Era

During the Zivrand Crisis, House Rikhiond harbored two major slavery posts on Zivrand, one at Tel Aruhn and one at Drilosual. The Rikhiond themselves refused to join in discussions on the outlawing of slavery, insisting that the right to own slaves was gauranteed through the Armistice.

House Rikhiond was also attempting rapid illegal expansion of their land, starting with Tel Vos. In response, both the Drogither and the Miazukrul sent out forces in attempts to fight back against the illegal settlements, though the Miazukrul were generally unsuccessful. House Rikhiond publicly denied supporting these settlements, though in reality, they supported their endeavors. During these times, House Rikhiond relied on Bosmer and Bretons for mercenary work. Within Rikhiond politics, certain members such as Master Aryon attempted to limit the power held by the old Rikhiond members such as Gothren and Neloth. With the aid of the Councilors of House Miazukrul, the Rikhiond also managed to invalidate the Mages Guild monopoly on the teachings of magic in Liakramar.

In regards to the Zidrodor himself, the Rikhiond Councillors were all not too concerned regarding the position, and most accepted without an issue. The only problem was with Archmagister Gothren, who refused to name the Zidrodor Ruavionis, and had to be killed instead.

## Neituand Crisis

While little is known of how the Rikhiond themselves fared during the Crisis, it is known that the Rikhiond on Zivrand tried their best to close the Neituand Gates, but to no avail.

It is also known that one of the Rikhiond intended to take over the Empire. Frathen Drothan had raised a private army, known as the Drothmeri Army, in an attempt to overthrow the Empire. In an attempt to hinder this attempt, someone had hired the Kutrinis to take out the commander of the army, known as Commander Telani Adrethi.

The loyalty of the men serving under Frathen was not great, with some considering him a madman. Most of the army only supported the cause of Frathen due to his high payments, and were planning on deserting his force as soon as he would actually go to battle. Eventually, the Drothmeri army was defeated by the Champion of Dhekovan, and Frathen Drothan's plans were stopped.

## Fourth Era

The Fourth Era saw Liakramar, and the Rikhiond, suffer under both the Red Year and the Argonian Invasion. Primarily the latter, with Argonians having successfully raided Rikhiond settlements, implied to have gone as far as Tel Vos. Their losses to the An-Xileel invaders even led some of the House to believe that the entire Great House had fallen.

Sometime after these events, House Rikhiond no longer had holdings on mainland Liakramar, only having possessions on Zivrand instead. While lacking in possessions, there were still several Wizard Lords within the House. In contrast to the other Wizard Lords, Master Neloth had Tel Mithryn on Phaidhodor as opposed to Zivrand.

## Dragon Crisis

House Rikhiond saw some developments during the Dragon Crisis of 4E 201, primarily on the isle of Phaidhodor under the command of Master Neloth. Within Thevaelass, Brelyna Maryon was one of the first Rikhiond to have left Liakramar in a long time, choosing to settle at the College of Winterhold to study conjuration magic. Ironically, Master Neloth proclaimed that Savos Aren, the Arch-Mage at Winterhold wouldn't be fit to be his apprentice, though Neloth did admit that Savos knew decent ward spells.

On Phaidhodor itself, Neloth experimented on the Heart Stones, which he believed arrived on the island with the eruption of Tuathon. Neloth sought to experiment with the Stones on living subjects, and because of it, required knowledge in regards to the Forsworn Briarhearts. Other research from Neloth allowed him to learn that heart stones located near ash will allow the ash to animate. These ash spawn ended up attacking Tel Mithryn, and it would turn out that their reanimation was the work of Ildari Sarothril, a former apprentice of Master Neloth. Ildari felt betrayed by Neloth due to her suffering during the Heart Stone experiment, and became increasingly paranoid afterwards, eventually even resurrecting the long-dead General Falx Carius.

Neloth would also come in contact with the Last Dragonborn during their quest to defeat Miraak. Neloth, in research on the Black Books, discovered the location of a Black Book hidden in a Tekreir ruin, which he was unable to obtain on his own. Through successful teamwork, the Dragonborn was able to read the Black Book, and learn its secrets. Following this, Neloth learned what the secrets the Black Book held, and returned to Tel Mithryn, believing he had learned a new way to locate Black Books.
