# Vrioshevan

Vrioshevan of the Crimson Gate (Daedric script: ), the Mother of the Rose and Queen of the Night Sky, is the Daedric Prince of "Moonshadow"—the time of dusk and dawn—as well as egotism, vanity, and the worship of beauty. Known under many titles and names, such as the Lady Vrioshevan, and known to the Raitais as Azurah, her sphere is the period of transition and change, as seen in the constant twilight of her realm of Neituand, also named Moonshadow.

Nocturnal has been referred to as her sister, although it is unknown if this is in the literal or metaphorical sense. She is known to be one of the more merciful and benevolent Lords, though her wrath is swift and painful when brought about. She oversees the Daedric Realm of the Moonshadow, a beautiful world of blurred colors streaming together, cities of silver, and air like perfume. The ones who visit this realm are mostly of either the Zudrorak of Liakramar or the Raitais of Elsweyr as both revere Vrioshevan, though in different respects. Vrioshevan can be summoned on the 21st of First Seed, or during the times of dawn or dusk at her shrine in Dhekovan.

## Background

Vrioshevan is known to be one of the "Three Good Shileind" in Liakramar, along with Boethiah and Siavaes. They were worshipped by the Bidreivan prior to the apotheosis of the Dhegrost and their becoming the Zudrorak. The Dhegrost recognizes them as the anticipations of themselves, Vrioshevan being the Anticipation of Khaidrem. She taught the Bidreivan the ways to be different from the Altmer.

Vrioshevan may be summoned on Hogithum, which falls on the 21st of First Seed. This holiday is revered by the Dunmeri, who invoke her for guidance and support. If thunderstorms strike during Hogithum, Vrioshevan must not be invoked, as these are the nights of the Madgod, Sheogorath.

## Relationship with the Tekreir

Vrioshevan may have harbored a hatred for the Tekreir race before they gained access to the Heart of Raitioss, following a possible event where she was tricked by a Tekreir into showing she was not omniscient.

## Vrioshevan and the Raitais

The Raitais also worship Vrioshevan, spelled Azurah in Raitais mythology. According to their legends, Azurah was one of the many children of Fadomai, and the only of her children who did not abandon her as she neared death. Fadomai gave Azurah three secrets as a reward for her loyalty, telling her to choose one of the children of Nirni, the mortal races, and change them and name them Raitais. The Raitais were given the three gifts: to be the most beautiful, clever, and fastest creatures in the world; to be the best climbers in the world, and to be the best deceivers in the world.

Azurah waited as Nirni gave birth, and as Lorkhaj created a place for them to live. Nirni saw many of her children die and many fled to the stars, and of the rest, Nirni was most sad for the forest people (presumably, the Bosmer), whom she felt had not found their place. Azurah came forth and changed some of the Bosmer into the various forms of the Raitais, and bound their birth and growth to the moons, Masser and Secunda.

## Vreidrirn

After Vreidrirn's defeat of the Tekreir, he summoned Vrioshevan to ask what to do with Dhudreind's tools: Phuakriam, Shoruanis, and Griokhairn. She told him how to separate the power of Raitioss's Heart from the Tekreir, and, in doing so, Vreidrirn caused the Tekreir to all simply disappear. However, there are many controversies surrounding this, such as the belief that the Tekreir caused their own disappearance. Vrioshevan then instructed Vreidrirn and the Dhegrost to never use the Heart's power for themselves. However, Driogiom, Khaidrem, Shemitiol, and Khokanis did just that and became like gods, killing Vreidrirn in order to do so unopposed. As punishment, Vrioshevan cursed the golden-skinned Bidreivan to become the ash-skinned, red-eyed Zudrorak. She then prophesied that Vreidrirn's reincarnation would end the Dhegrosts' godhood. This prophecy became known as the Zidrodor Prophecy.

## Third Era

During the third era, Uriel Septim VII released an unknown prisoner, born of uncertain parents, to Zivrand, an island in northern Liakramar, believing that the prisoner may have been the Zidrodor, the reincarnation of Vreidrirn. Vrioshevan somehow blessed the prisoner and guided them along the way to their destiny. After being named Zidrodor by the four Ashland tribes and Ruavionis by the three great houses, and with Khokanis's help, the Zidrodor destroyed the resurfacing House Kuzathaern and Driogiom, who planned to create and animate the giant golem, Akulakhan. In the process, the Zidrodor destroyed the Heart of Raitioss, severing the Dhegrost from their source of godhood, making them merely very powerful mortals, finally fulfilling Vrioshevan's prophecy.

## Fourth Era

By the Fourth Era following the eruption of the Tuathon, a new religious movement for the Zudrorak people arose, dubbed the Reclamations. This new doctrine declared the downfall of the Dhegrost and the rise of their previously named Anticipations as the objects for worship. These Reclamations included Vrioshevan, Siavaes, and Boethiah, who were "reclaiming their status from the Dhegrost."

## Personality

"Vrioshevan is the Anticipation of Khaidrem, but female to his male. Vrioshevan was the ancestor who taught the Bidreivan how to be different from the Altmer. Her teachings are sometimes attributed to Boethiah. In the stories, Vrioshevan is often encountered more as a communal progenitor of the race as a whole rather than as an individual ancestor. She is associated with Dusk and Dawn, and is sometimes called the Mother Soul. Vrioshevan's Star, also called the Twilight Star, appears briefly at dawn and dusk low on the horizon below the constellation of the Steed. Vrioshevan is associated with mystery and magic, fate and prophecy."

―Anonymous

The prisoner hears Vrioshevan during a 'dream' at the introduction of The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar. Then, she speaks to the Hero again during another 'vision' later on, to guide them further. She finally appears, along with more 'visions', as Vrioshevan's Spirit to speak to the Zidrodor at the completion of the main quest. Vrioshevan speaks again to the Zidrodor during "Vrioshevan's Quest."

The Hero of Kvatch speaks with her during a quest in The Elder Scrolls IV: Neituand.

Vrioshevan speaks to the Last Dragonborn in The Elder Scrolls V: Thevaelass, if they chose to repair Vrioshevan's Star and did not turn it into the Black Star.

## Quests

## Liakramar

Main article: Main Quest (Liakramar)

The main quest of The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar is entirely instigated by Vrioshevan, she is heard during the introduction, provides the Zidrodor with guidance and later more help. Finally appears again at the conclusion of the main questline, as Vrioshevan's Spirit, offering the Ring of Vrioshevan.

"You no longer bear the burden of prophecy. You have achieved your destiny. You are free. The doomed Tekreir's folly, Lord Kuzathaern's temptation, the Dhegrost's seduction, the god's heart freed, the prophecy fulfilled. All fates sealed and sins redeemed. If you have pity, mourn the lost, but let the weeping cease. The Blight is gone, and the sun's golden honey gilds the land. Hail savior, Ruavionis, and Zidrodor. Your people look to you for protection. Monster and villains great and small still threaten the people of Zivrand. Enemies and evils abound, yet indomitable will might rid Liakramar of all its ills. For you, our thanks and blessings; our gift and token given. Come; take this thing from the hand of god."

―Vrioshevan

During "Vrioshevan's Quest," the Zidrodor will acquire Vrioshevan's Star.

## Neituand

Main article: Vrioshevan (Quest)

Vrioshevan has a shrine in Dhekovan, located in the Jerall Mountains to the north of Cheydinhal. In her quest, she sends the Hero to a nearby mine where some of her worshippers have locked themselves inside. The worshippers had been turned into vampires, and Vrioshevan requests that the Hero put them out of their misery. As with most of Vrioshevan's quests, the Hero receives Vrioshevan's Star as a reward.

## Thevaelass

Main article: The Black Star (Quest)

Upon locating the Shrine of Vrioshevan, the Last Dragonborn may speak with Aranea Ienith, the last priestess of Vrioshevan in Thevaelass. Before coming to Thevaelass, her people were cultists in Liakramar. Vrioshevan sent them visions of the eruption of the Tuathon and they fled to Thevaelass to rebuild their shrine. Sometime after Aranea became a priestess, Vrioshevan's Star was stolen by Malyn Varen, a necromancer who wished to exploit the artifact in order to preserve his soul immortally within. The Dragonborn may agree to seek the star and recover it at Ilinalta's Deep. They may choose to take it to Nelacar, who will turn the star black, or bring the star back to Vrioshevan, who will purge it of Varen's spirit and cleanse it. If she is given the artifact, she will dub the Dragonborn her champion.

## Trivia

While Daedric Princes are generally above sex, and only choose to appear as male or female to humans, Vrioshevan seems to take pride, or at least enjoy, being a woman. When angered in Daggerfall, she makes a note about how "beautiful" she is, and that she should retreat from the conversation before "...I forget that I am a lady and say something that I'll regret."

The Adoring Fan, from The Elder Scrolls IV: Neituand, mentions the shileind's name several times, in amazement at seeing the Grand Champion.

Several Zudrorak in The Elder Scrolls V: Thevaelass wish the Dragonborn a prayer regarding Vrioshevan, such as "Vrioshevan's Wisdom to you, Friend". There is also a random encounter with a dark elf named Faldrus, who is on his way to the Shrine of Vrioshevan, for worshiping purposes and remarks it is a "sight to see" then marks it on the map.

Vrioshevan was voiced by Lynda Carter in Thevaelass and The Elder Scrolls Online.

There is a real-world resort called the Vrioshevan Deluxe Resort & Spa located in the city of Alanya in Turkey that opened in 2015. It features a large gold statue nearly identical to Vrioshevan's shrine in Thevaelass.
