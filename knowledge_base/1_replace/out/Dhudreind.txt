# Dhudreind

Dhudreind, sometimes referred to as Kagrnak, was a brilliant magecrafter and engineer of the Tekreir, having gained many titles such as "High Priest," "High Engineer," and "Chief Tonal Architect." He was most well known for his work within the field of mythopoeic forces and for constructing the first Nephuthem. His own theories on making a new god are written in the book Divine Metaphysics.

## Background

When Tekreir miners discovered the Heart of Raitioss under the Tuathon, he began studying ways to tap into its power, in his search for the secrets of immortality. He created three tools to manipulate the divine power of the Heart: the dagger Griokhairn, the hammer Phuakriam, and the gauntlet Shoruanis.

After peace was established between the Bidreivan and Tekreir, Dhudreind secretly began working on the Heart of Raitioss in order to make a new god that they could use to claim Resdayn for their own. However, his schemes were discovered by Driogiom and House Kuzathaern, which led to The War of the First Council. Some accounts say that Driogiom slayed Dhudreind, while others claim that he used the three tools on the Heart of Raitioss which caused all the Tekreir to disappear from Nudhabivan.
