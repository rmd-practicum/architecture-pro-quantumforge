# House Kuzathaern

House Kuzathaern, the Draedhiost House, Shadow House, Sleeping House, Lost House, the Tribe Unmourned or "unmourned house", was one of the Great Houses of Liakramar. Ruled by Driogiom, known as Voryn Kuzathaern in ancient times, the crest of the house is an insect.

## Members

Generic members of the Great House were the following.

Ascended Sleepers, powerful sorcerers of the House.

Ash Ghouls, a type of Ash creature, with experience in magic.

Ash slaves, slaves of the Draedhiost House, possessing knowledge in shock magic.

Ash zombies, undead members of the House.

Lame Taeriboth, powerful, but slow, taeriboth creature.

Taeriboth Stalkers, victims of the taeriboth disease.

## Beliefs

"The Draedhiost House cannot be restored without war. Enlightenment cannot grow without the risk of upsetting the tradition-bound and complacent herd."

―Voryn Kuzathaern

Little is known of the beliefs of House Kuzathaern. In terms of tradition, it is House custom for the Kuzathaerns to allow a challenger to strike the first blow during combat. During the Third Era, the members of the House welcomed all "true Zudrorak", and aimed to "drive the n'wah" from Liakramar. The Blight was created by Lord Driogiom, and aimed to kill foreigners in Liakramar. The ones opposing Lord Kuzathaern would die from the disease, while those who would follow him, would instead find strength and healing. The Great House also opposed the usage of necromancy, as well as vampires.

Further aims of the House was removing all individuals of inferior and mongrel races from the province, driving the Empire out, and reclaim lands lost to Thevaelass and Argonia. Followers of House Kuzathaern believed that the House was falsely denounced as the house of traitors, and the other Houses attempted to destroy the House for eternity.

## Religion

The goal of the Draedhiost House during the Third Era was to establish a theocracy under worship of Akulakhan, the Second Nephuthem. The god-priests of this new religion would be the ancient heirs of House Kuzathaern, with the House itself becoming the new dominant power in the province. With the rise of this new religion, House Kuzathaern also aimed to remove the Dhegrost Temple from power. The worship of Akulakhan was meant to be extended to every nation of Nudhabivan. Within the shrines of the Draedhiost House, Ash Statues could often be found, alongside various offerings, as well as bells of the Draedhiost House, including the Draedhiost House Bell Hammer used to ring them.

Akulakhan would serve a number of purposes, primarily it would serve as the champion of the armies of House Kuzathaern, first liberating Zivrand, and then Liakramar, with possibly the rest of Nudhabivan following afterward. It would also serve as a sower and cultivator of the divine power obtained from the Heart of Raitioss. Its final purpose was to serve as the banner and symbol of the cause of House Kuzathaern—defying the Empire, liberating mortals from ancient superstitions, and to glorify the crusade against the gods.

After having taken control, the plan for the Draedhiost House was to form the elite cadre of Driogiom's movement. Cultists would expand depending on their enlightenment, becoming either holy warriors or priests. The duty of these warriors and priests would be to prepare themselves for service, while their joy and liberation would be learning more of the divine dreamworld.

## Recruitment

The Draedhiost House recruited their followers during the Third Era through dreams. One of the major bases of operation for this ordeal were the ruins of Kogoruhn, with smaller bases being in port villages and the waterfront districts in Khokanis. Other recruitment aimed to go through smuggling syndicates, but also willing recruitment from disaffected populations, such as the poor and people with anti-Imperial sympathies.

This form of recruitment would continue to expand, with new bases in regular towns and villages, as well as abandoned structures such as ruins or towers. Corrupted cultists would also be trained as raiders and irregular troops for the House, while political resistance was fought back as much as possible. Through assassinations and terror, the House also aimed to weaken, distract, and disrupt the Imperial Legions stationed within Liakramar, as well as their Drogither allies. In doing so, inspiring uprisings by the native poor against rich and powerful foreigners, all the while Sleepers and Dreamers would work on Akulakhan.

With new cultists, the House gained new followers, with taeriboth beasts being created from weaker cultists, whereas the stronger ones became Ascended Sleepers.

## History

## First Era

During the First Era, House Kuzathaern, specifically Driogiom, provided Vulutarak Vreidrirn proof that lord Dhudreind had discovered the Heart of Raitioss, and was tapping into its power. With this power, Dhudreind was creating a new God which was a powerful and fearsome weapon of its own. Vreidrirn would then travel to Holamayan, asking Vrioshevan for guidance, he learned that what Driogiom said was the truth. As a result, Vreidrirn opted for war.

During their battle with the Tekreir, Driogiom, Vreidrirn, and a number of soldiers fought against the Tekreir in Tuathon, until the Tekreir disappeared. Driogiom proposed destroying Dhudreind's Tools, hoping to prevent the tools from falling in the wrong hands. After changing words with the Dhegrost, it was decided to keep the Tools by Vreidrirn and the Dhegrost. However, when Vrioshevan was being summoned, the Dhegrost murdered Vreidrirn. When the Dhegrost entered Tuathon, the Bidreivan had changed into Zudrorak, and Driogiom had noticed this change. Seeking to avenge the death of Vreidrirn, he fought back, but to no avail. Driogiom was driven off and presumed dead, though it has also been said that Vreidrirn himself struck down Driogiom.

Other accounts state that House Kuzathaern was allied with the Tekreir, and were instead defeated for their treachery by the other Great Houses. The remaining members of the House were either slain or adopted into the other Great Houses.

## Second Era

Ever since 2E 882 Driogiom and his kin have been awake, moving into the Heart Chamber of Tuathon. Driogiom uses a ritual to bind himself, as well as high brethren as heartwight, in a ritual of his own devising. Construction of Akulakhan also begins, with the heartwights and atronachs creating a chamber near the Heart of Raitioss. When the Dhegrost arrived that same year for their annual ritual to use the Heart's power, the Draedhiost House ambushed them, and drove them away.

The awakening of Driogiom and his kin is said to have played a role in the signing of the Armistice, as Tiber Septim was hesitant to fight the Dhegrost and having to deal with Driogiom.

## Third Era

Ever since 2E 882, several Dhegrost campaigns assault the Tuathon, seeking to force access into the Heart Chamber, however, the Draedhiost House successfully bested them back at every assault.

In 3E 400, Kuzathaern Uthol successfully reoccupies Kogoruhn and fortifies it as an advance base for the Draedhiost House. The Blight storms becoming more frequent, and soul sickness spreads in the regions close to the Tuathon. Ten years later the village of Gnaar Mok and the waterfront districts of Khokanis housed Draedhiost House bases, with operatives from the House also exploiting smugglers to spread their influence.

As of 3E 415, every town in Zivrand had small cells of followers of the Draedhiost House, with the larger operations being centralized in remote dungeons, where creatures are bred and the followers are trained. A mere two years later, Kuzathaern Odros and Kuzathaern Vemyn manage to take the artifacts of Phuakriam and Griokhairn from Shemitiol and Khaidrem, with both the Tribunes being rescued by Khokanis. While the Temple and Buoyant Armigers are aware that Shemitiol and Khaidrem were forced to retreat, nobody was certain what damage had been done to them. The Temple recognizes something has happened, but does not take the problem seriously. In the years 3E 426 and 427, Draedhiost House assassinations occurred against prominent Imperial citizens and members of House Drogither. The House itself becoming more aggressive, starting attacks by both cultists and victims from soul sickness.

During the late-Third Era, followers of House Kuzathaern believed that Liakramar had grown weak, and the Zudrorak were no more than slaves ruled by foreigners. They aimed to create a Liakramar for the "free and proud Zudrorak." Within Khokanis, the Dreamer Prophet was responsible for the killings of a number of foreigners, as well as two Gaimaths for the House. The people of Zivrand suffered from the sleepers, who would often engage with non-Zudrorak in combat, wielding clubs. When arrested, they provided nothing of value, only mumbling about Driogiom and the Draedhiost House.

The Dhegrost Temple attempted to fight back against the Draedhiost House, but suffered numerous defeats, and lost artifacts. Gaimath Feril Salmyn aimed to discover the location of Kogoruhn, but was killed there while wearing the Hair Shirt of Saint Aralor. The Cleaver of St. Felms would also be lost by a Buoyant Armiger at the Draedhiost House base of Tureynulal. Another Buoyant Armiger would lose the Crosier of St. Llothis in the ruins of Tuathon itself. The Gaimaths also failed in retrieving the Robe of St. Roris from Assemanu, a Draedhiost House base. The Temple did launch one successful raid on a Draedhiost House base, namely at Hassour. The Imperial Legion was also involved in fighting the Draedhiost House, though their only recorded event, the raid on the shrine at Ilunibi, failed.

## Zidrodor

During the journey to defeat Driogiom, the Zidrodor was asked on several occasions to join Driogiom and the Draedhiost House by the members of the House. They also attempted to make the Zidrodor question the Daedric Prince Vrioshevan's prophecy.

The Dhegrost had proposed a five-step plan to defeat Driogiom. First, there would be a series of aggressive raids in order to scout within the Ghostfence, with the Buoyant Armigers and Gaimaths providing the Zidrodor with aid. After the scouting and raiding, there would be raids on the Heartwights of House Kuzathaern, while also recovering powerful enchanted items from their bodies. Having successfully done so, raids on Vemynal and Odrosal would take place to take back Phuakriam and Griokhairn. At last, a main assault would take place on Tuathon itself.

The Zidrodor would work with the Thiakraen, Zakraezails, as well as the Great Houses of Drogither, Miazukrul, and Rikhiond to complete the Zidrodor Prophecies. Troubles would arise in being named Ruavionis of each of the three Houses. House Miazukrul originally refused to accept the Zidrodor as Ruavionis due to believing him to be an Imperial spy, and Bolvyn Venim's dislike of outlanders. House Rikhiond was largely indifferent, but Archmagister Gothren refused to name the Zidrodor Ruavionis, as he believed the Zidrodor was not the true incarnate. Within House Drogither, Orvas Dren was a follower of the Draedhiost House, and held the authority over two other Drogither Councillors, blocking the Zidrodor from easily being named Ruavionis. After having completed all the steps, and obtaining Phuakriam, Griokhairn, and Shoruanis, the Zidrodor had to face Driogiom within Tuathon itself. Here the two would originally fight, until Driogiom was defeated. However, Driogiom regained his lifeforce due to the power of the Heart of Raitioss, posing a threat to the Zidrodor in the Heart Chamber. Eventually the Zidrodor would destroy the Heart of Raitioss, and destroy Driogiom and the Draedhiost House.
