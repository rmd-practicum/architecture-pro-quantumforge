# Zidrodor

"You are the Zidrodor. You are the Chosen. You can't fail."

―Ghosts of failed Zidrodors to the Zidrodor.

Zidrodor, also known as the Protector of Liakramar and Vreidrirn-Born-Again, is the reincarnation of Vulutarak Vreidrirn as well as the player character and protagonist of The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar, and its expansions Bloodmoon and Dhegrost.

## Background

## Vrioshevan's Declaration of Vengeance

"You want to chide me... why did I murder Vreidrirn? Why did I break my oath to Vrioshevan not to use Dhudreind's tools?"

―Khokanis

In the aftermath of the Battle of Tuathon, either as punishment for using Dhudreind's Tools or for killing Vreidrirn, Vrioshevan proclaimed that Vreidrirn would return to punish the Dhegrost and right their wrongs.

Over time, the Dhegrost Temple would persecute those who believed that Vreidrirn would return. Thus, knowledge of what the Dhegrost had done and pre-Dhegrost worship dwindled. Despite this, several Zakraezails continued to believe in Vreidrirn's return despite opposition from the Temple. Several people appeared claiming to be the Zidrodor, but none fulfilled the Zidrodor Prophecy.

## Events of Liakramar

"This is where you get off. Come with me."

―Imperial Guard

In 3E 427, the Zidrodor, a prisoner born to uncertain parents under a certain sign, was sent by Uriel Septim VII to Liakramar, and arrived in the port town of Seyda Neen. This was done to set the prisoner up as a member of the Thiakraen, and fulfill the Zidrodor prophecies in such a way as to benefit the Empire.

Upon arrival in Seyda Neen, the prisoner is ordered to deliver a coded package to Caius Cosades, which contains information about their role and identity. The Zidrodor is then tasked with finding information about the Zidrodor Cult, the Draedhiost House and the Dissident Priests, all of which provide information about the Zidrodor.

During the course of this, the prisoner meets with the Urshilaku Zakraezails, who provide them with a series of tests which allow the prisoner to become the Zidrodor.

The trials which the Zidrodor has to pass are as follows:

On a certain day to uncertain parents Incarnate moon and star reborn. This relates to the Zidrodor's birth on a certain day to uncertain parents, and is fulfilled by virtue of the character's birth.

Neither blight nor age can harm him. The Curse-of-Flesh before him flies. During the course of their travels, the Zidrodor meets Kuzathaern Gares, who curses them with taeriboth disease. Divayth Fyr then removes the symptoms of the disease, although the Zidrodor still has the disease, which renders them ageless.

In caverns dark Vrioshevan's eye sees. And makes to shine the moon and star. At Sul-Matuul's bidding, the Zidrodor travels to the Cavern of the Incarnate and recovers Vreidrirn's ring.

A stranger's voice unites the Houses. Three Halls call him Ruavionis. In the face of mounting threats from Driogiom, the Zidrodor convinces members of Houses Drogither, Miazukrul and Rikhiond to make them ruavionis, a war leader to fight Driogiom.

A stranger's hand unites the Velothi. Four Tribes call him Zidrodor. From the proofs of the previous trials undertaken, the Ahemmusa, Erabenimsun, Urshilaku and Zainab tribes proclaim the prisoner the Zidrodor.

He honors blood of the tribe unmourned. He eats their sin, and is reborn. The Zidrodor defeats a variety of enemies from the Draedhiost House, culminating in raids on their citadels around Tuathon and defeating Driogiom and his Ash Vampire kin.

His mercy frees the cursed false gods, Binds the broken, redeems the mad. During the final battle in Tuathon, the Zidrodor destroys the Tekreir enchantments on the Heart of Raitioss, rendering the Dhegrost mortal again, before killing Shemitiol, who had murdered Khaidrem.

## The Bloodmoon

At some point during their time on Nudhabivan, the Zidrodor traveled to the frozen wasteland of Phaidhodor, after having heard rumors of a downtrodden Imperial fort, only to get wrapped up in the chaos of Hircine's Hunt. After Fort Frostmoth is attacked by Werewolves the Zidrodor travels to Skaal Village and fulfils the Bloodskal trials. Being accepted into the clan as a friend the signs of the Bloodmoon prophecy.

The Coming of the Hounds, (as sign forewarning of Werewolves on Phaidhodor.)

Fire from the Eye of Glass, (the great Lake Fjalding grows a large inferno atop the ice. Although some say a Draugr Lord was causing the flame)

Tide of Woe, (the sign telling of a great Horker Massacre on the coast of Phaidhodor.)

The Bloodmoon, (the final sign depicting Secunda turning blood red.)

At the end of this Hircine in his mortal form battles the Zidrodor who in turn wins and causes Mortrag Glacier, the glacier they were battling in, to collapse and hearing Hircine's voice curse the Zidrodor and pledge that he will walk the land again.

## Fate

Following the fulfillment of the prophecies, the Zidrodor vanished. In 3E 433, there are rumors that the Zidrodor has left on an expedition to Akavir, and has not been heard from since their departure.

## Trivia

In The Elder Scrolls V: Dragonborn, Neloth seemingly confirms the Zidrodor's gender is male. Former writer Michael Kirkbride later claimed, "That line was a mistake a designer made in haste. Consider it a glitch." though it should be noted that Kirkbride had not worked on the Dragonborne DLC personally.
