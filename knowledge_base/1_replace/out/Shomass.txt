# Shomass

"Shomass is the district seat of House Drogither, and the largest settlement on Zivrand after Khokanis City."

―Anonymous

Shomass, Stoneforest, or Stonewood is one of the four major cities located in the Zivrand District of Liakramar and is the capital of both the West Gash and the Drogither District in Zivrand. Shomass is a very prosperous city that gets traction from places such as Khokanis City and Nuamioss.

## Description

## Geography

Shomass is located on the northern banks of the Odai River and is built on the river's east and west shores. Shomass's main feature is the large canal constructed in the middle of town; this canal is used for fishing with the city walls as well as a mode of transport throughout the Odai Riverbanks. Shomass is also built in between small mountains that give it a natural barrier to protect it from incoming weather (i.e. Ashstorms). Shomass is located on the Western Main Road that goes through the West Gash and leads to the Ascadian Isles and the Zivrand Ashlands. Miners come and go from Shomass since many Mines exist in the area such as the Vassir-Didanat Ebony Mine and the Caldera Ebony Mine in the Caldera Crater.

Within the inner walls, Shomass is divided into two, but in reality, Shomass consists of three districts. The eastern side of the river is known as Labor Town and it is where many of the residents live. People ranging from the middle class to the lower class live in Shomass, many of Shomass's criminal aspect resides in Labor Town. The area on the western shore is known as the Commercial District. The Commercial District is largely where all the business happens, the local Fighters Guild and Mages Guild are found in here, as well as the Silt Strider. The Silt Strider in Shomass can take travelers to places such as Nuamioss, Seyda Neen, Suran, and Khokanis City. High Town is the westernmost district and is located on the hills of Caldera Crater. This is where Shomass's nobility lives as well as the Temple formerly dedicated to the Dhegrost; it now worships the House of Reclamations.

## Cuisine

Shomass Blue – An alcoholic beverage that is banned in various provinces such as Thevaelass. This concoction is made with Moon Sugar which may be the reason why it is banned since consuming too much Moon Sugar can lead to addictions.

Shomass Cabbage Biscuits – Made with Greens and Flour. Shomass Cabbage Biscuits is an excellent meal that has received critical acclaim from places all across Liakramar.

## History

## Second Era

Main article: Shomass (Online)

During the Interregnum in 2E 583, House Miazukrul claimed a majority of Western Zivrand and built the city of Shomass with the Drogither style in mind. Shomass became the Miazukrul Capital of Zivrand and settlements such as Gnisis and Ald Velothi followed suit with the Miazukrul Style in mind. The Counselor to rule Shomass was Councilor Eris Releth and had connections among the higher-ups in House Miazukrul. However, his dedication to House Miazukrul made him grow distant from his children, Veya Releth and Ulvan Releth, this proved to be his downfall. The civilized Dunmeri-folk have a hatred directed to the Zakraezails that roam Liakramar's Deadlands and prevent entrance into their modern cities. Ulvan Releth was a very kind person who had respect for the Zakraezails in the West Gash, which gave Eris some concerns that Ulvan may defect from Miazukrul. Eris used Captain Brivan to order a soldier to kill an Zakraezail Merchant to see whether Ulvan would intervene or not. Ulvan intercepted the Soldier and killed him in haste. This caused Ulvan to be exiled from Zivrand, but he would go to the local Zakraezails instead.

Knowing this, Brivan would use Ferhara's Warclaws to kill the Zakraezails and subsequently Ulvan. Veya grew suspicious of this and ran away from home to uncover Ulvan's disappearances. With the help of Kutrinis Mentor and a mysterious traveler, she was able to uncover Ulvan's assassination and her father's role in this. She sought vengeance against Brivan and her father, and she assassinated both of them. Her fate was unknown but regardless, Veya Releth disappeared forever.

Since the city of Shomass is under the control of House Miazukrul, the Shomass region is also under the control of the Ebonheart Pact, an alliance created in the aftermath of the Second Akaviri Invasion. Although, the Pact did not have any notable headquarters in Zivrand.

## Third Era

During the Imperial Simulacrum from 3E 389 to 3E 399, Emperor Uriel Septim VII was imprisoned along with Talin Warhaft in the Deadlands by Jagar Tharn. Tharn planned for months to capture the throne, and he was able to achieve so in the span of ten years. Tharn had a powerful weapon called the Staff of Chaos, and he split it into nine pieces and scattered them across Nudhabivan, the staff was the only thing keeping Uriel Septim and Warhaft in Neituand. A warrior escaped his clutches and traveled all corners of Nudhabivan to retrieve the missing pieces. The hero was known as the Eternal Champion, and he assembled the staff and defeated Jagar Tharn at the Imperial Palace. The Eternal Champion had at one point visited the town of Stoneforest in their quest to recover the pieces.

In 3E 426, a tax revolt enveloped the city of Shomass, and it raised concerns in the Drogither Council. Luckily, the revolt was quelled down without any violence needed. The Drogither Council sent notes to the then Duke Vedam Dren of Ebonheart about the situation, to give him a reason to lower taxes.

In 3E 427, Shomass prospered along with the other three cities in Zivrand. The city was under the rule of Nileno Dorvayn, the only leader to not be a Councilor of her own Great Houses. Regardless, she was able to manage Shomass for several years. In the same year, the Zidrodor Prophecy came to fruition, and the Zidrodor roamed Zivrand to fulfill the prophecy. The warrior traveled to Shomass and met with Dorvayn to gather the Drogither Councilors to become the Ruavionis of Drogither.

## Fourth Era

In 4E 5, Tuathon, which is located in the center of Zivrand, had erupted after centuries later. The resulting eruption caused the destruction of virtually all of Zivrand's Settlements including Shomass, Drilosual, Khokanis City, and Nuamioss. A House Miazukrul Councilor in Nuakhaivan started a relief effort to rebuild the settlements on Zivrand. As of 4E 201, it is unknown whether the effort reached Shomass and the city is rebuilt.
