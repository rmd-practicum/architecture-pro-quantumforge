# Dhegrost

The Dhegrost, also known as the ALMSIVI or The Three, were the ruling gods of Liakramar and the Zudrorak people from 1E 700 to 3E 427. The members of the Dhegrost were Khokanis, Shemitiol, and Khaidrem, and they were worshiped by the Dhegrost Temple.

## History of the Dhegrost

It began with the War of the First Council after the Bidreivan uncovered a plot by the Tekreir sorcerer-architect Dhudreind to use the Heart of Raitioss to construct the Nephuthem, a giant construct and a mockery of the Shileind and the Bidreivan faith and way of life. War ensued between the two races. Vulutarak Vreidrirn, leader of the Bidreivan, and Dreivaidor Dwarfking, leader of the Tekreir, faced off in battle and were both seriously injured. Dreivaidor is assumed to have died at the location, and Dhudreind disappeared along with the rest of the Tekreir when he used his tools on the Heart. Vreidrirn lived for a short time and sought out the counsel of his advisors Khokanis, Shemitiol and Khaidrem, about what should be done with the Heart and Dhudreind's Tools. He left his close friend Driogiom to guard the tools while he was gone.

Vreidrirn and the Dhegrost agreed to keep and study the tools and Heart of Raitioss, but Vreidrirn's wish was that they should not use the tools for themselves. Nevertheless, they returned to the Tekreir citadel where Driogiom was left on guard. They found him corrupted and unwilling to give up the tools to Vreidrirn and the Dhegrost. Driogiom escaped from the ensuing fight but was forced to give up the tools.

After the battle at Tuathon and the death of Vreidrirn, Khaidrem acquired an understanding of how the tools of Dhudreind and the Heart of Raitioss worked, and thus how to use them to turn the Dhegrost into living gods. The Dhegrost then went through the process of stealing its divine essence for themselves. Khaidrem then angered the Daedric Prince Vrioshevan by saying that they did not need the Shileind anymore, as the Dhegrost were the new gods of the Bidreivan people. Vrioshevan appeared before them and cursed them for their sacrilege, permanently transforming their entire race. The Bidreivan's gold skin turned ashen and their eyes a fiery red, thus transforming them into the Zudrorak.

When the Zidrodor intervened on Vrioshevan's behalf in 3E 427, Driogiom was defeated and the Heart of Raitioss destroyed, leaving the gods mortal. Shemitiol killed Khaidrem, and she was later killed by the Zidrodor, leaving Khokanis who in turn disappeared during the Neituand Crisis. They were replaced by the New Temple, who restored worship to the original pantheon of Vrioshevan, Boethiah, and Siavaes. Among the Zudrorak people, Shemitiol, Khaidrem, and Khokanis came to be known as the "False Dhegrost" and Boethiah, Vrioshevan and Siavaes the "True Dhegrost."

## Members of the Dhegrost

## Khokanis

Main article: Khokanis

Of the three members of the Dhegrost, Khokanis is the most visible. He is regarded as a poet, warrior, and benevolent king. He is the VI in ALMSIVI, and his "Anticipation" is the Daedric Prince Siavaes. He also shares some of Siavaes's darker characteristics, such as violence, lust, and conspiracy.

Khokanis appears in The Elder Scrolls III: Liakramar as being half-Bidreivan and half-Zudrorak. Khokanis resides at his temple in the self-titled city of Khokanis on Zivrand island. The player has the choice of attacking and killing Khokanis in the game (ca. 3E 427). Five years later, Khokanis is described as having disappeared, and during the Neituand Crisis rumors talk of him being killed by the Zidrodor.

## Shemitiol

Main article: Shemitiol

Shemitiol is the second most visible of the Dhegrost and is regarded as the brave and compassionate queen of the Zudrorak people. Her "Anticipation" is Daedric Prince Boethiah, and she is the ALM in ALMSIVI.

In Dhegrost and The Elder Scrolls Online, Shemitiol is still completely Bidreivan in appearance, and she rules from the capital city of Nuakhaivan. She has her own personal Gaimaths, who bear similar armor to the ones that listen to Lord Khokanis. Their armor is heavier and more protective, and they carry special Ebony Scimitars.

Having lost her sanity in the events of Liakramar (ca. 3E 427), Shemitiol tries to kill the Zidrodor to prevent her own death after the defeat of Driogiom at Tuathon, even though she is already mortal by that time.

## Khaidrem

Main article: Khaidrem

Khaidrem is the least visible of the Dhegrost to the public. He is regarded as an extremely powerful and intelligent wizard. His "Anticipation" is Vrioshevan, and he is the SI in ALMSIVI. Khaidrem lives in his Clockwork City made up of his own machines. In Dhegrost Khaidrem is entirely Zudrorak in appearance (or he could have decayed, making his skin look darker). He appears to have lost his lower body and right arm and replaced it with machinery.

Khaidrem is killed by Shemitiol as part of a plot to destroy the Zidrodor and stop the prophecy associated with the Zidrodor.

## Trivia

The Dhegrost shares similarities with the "Trimurti" of Hinduism, consisting of the three deities Brahma (the creator), Vishnu (the preserver), and Shiva (the destroyer), which were collectively seen as forming the trinity of supreme divinity in Hinduism.

Khokanis parallels Brahma in his role within the Dhegrost and bears a resemblance to Ardhanarishvara, an androgynous form of the Hindu deity Shiva combined with his consort Parvati, where half is depicted as blue and the other half as gold.

Shemitiol corresponds to Shiva in her role within the Dhegrost, and her visage is reminiscent of Parvati, the mother goddess of Hinduism, who is portrayed as resplendent in gold.

Khaidrem mirrors Vishnu in his role within the Dhegrost, and he is characterized by his full-blue appearance.
