# Drilosual

"Drilosual is large, with many craftsmen, traders, and trainers, but it is open only to Rikhiond retainers; outsiders should confine themselves to the Gateway Inn, and to Wolverine Hall, the Imperial quarters of the Legion garrison and guilds."

―Anonymous

Drilosual or Mushroom Forest is one of the four major cities located in the Zivrand District of Liakramar and is the capital of both Vrioshevan's Coast and the Rikhiond District of Zivrand. Drilosual is a city of absolute wonder as the spiraling mushroom towers kiss the skies above.

## Description

## Geography

Drilosual occupies one of the many islands that inhabit the Zafirbel Bay of Vrioshevan's Coast in Zivrand, and it occupies one of the bigger ones located in the east. Drilosual is only accessible by the sea, so a boat is required to enter Drilosual unless you are willing to swim all the way there. This can be quite tedious as the closest settlements on land would be the towns of Vos and Molag Mar. Drilosual itself is surrounded by the Rikhiond Mushroom Tower residing in the middle, Tel Naga is the original settlement as everything else was most likely built after Tel Naga's growth. Tel Naga is the biggest structure in Drilosual and is the home of the Archmagister of the Zivrand Rikhiond Council.

House Rikhiond is infamous for rejecting foreigners from their compounds such as Drilosual and Port Rikhionds. This is evident in Drilosual with the existence of the Gateway Inn, a large inn built on the western wall of Drilosual, as a lounging area for foreign visitors and is usually where many Non-Zudrorak people are found. South of the city is Wolverine Hall, an Imperial fortress designed to protect Drilosual from incoming invaders and to host the local Fighters Guild and Mages Guild chapters in Drilosual. Upon entering Drilosual via the Gateway Inn, you are introduced to the Market Square, bustling with local fisherman and artisans that pay tribute to the leaders of Drilosual.

## History

## Second Era

Main article: Drilosual (Online)

During the Interregnum in 2E 583, the Great House Rikhiond refused to join the other Great Houses in the Ebonheart Pact, this was done to keep the slave trade flowing within in their territories. This included Drilosual and the settlements of Vrioshevan's Coast.
History was made in Drilosual when an Argonian slave named Sun-in-Shadow was freed by an Outlander called the Vestige. Sun-in-Shadow was the first Argonian to become initiated into the ranks of the Great House Rikhiond. While she and Vestige tried to maintain her freedom as well as ensure her success, her fellow slave, Eoki planned to escape back to Black Marsh with the help of the Ropefish Gang at Vassamsi Grotto. Sun-in-Shadow wanted to have Eoki stay with her, but that would continue to make him slave while, Eoki wanted Sun-in-Shadow to leave with him to Black Marsh, their ancestral home. Eoki's fate was left unknown, but Sun-in-Shadow remained. She resided in the newly constructed Tel Vos until her final days.

## Third Era

Main article: Drilosual (Liakramar)

In 3E 427, the city of Drilosual prospered along with the other three cities in Zivrand. The city was under the rule of Master Neloth, an arrogant young mer that is capable of doing anything to achieve a higher status. In the same year, the Zidrodor Prophecy came to fruition, and the Zidrodor roamed Zivrand to fulfill their destiny of uniting the people of Liakramar to oppose the Dhegrost. The warrior traveled to Tel Naga of Drilosual to convince Neloth to give them the title of Ruavionis of the Great House Rikhiond.

## Fourth Era

In 4E 5, Tuathon, which is located in the center of Zivrand, had erupted after years of remaining dormant throughout Dunmeri history. The resulting explosion caused the destruction of virtually all of Zivrand's settlements including Shomass, Drilosual, Khokanis City, and Nuamioss. Very little life remained in the now endless sea of ash formerly known as Zivrand. A House Miazukrul Councilman from Nuakhaivan issued a relief effort to rebuild the cities of Zivrand from the ground up. As of 4E 201, it is unknown whether the effort reached Drilosual and the city was rebuilt.

Prior to the eruption, Neloth left the city of Drilosual to establish his private tower on the southern coast of the Hirstaang Forest of Phaidhodor. Tel Mithryn was built after the eruption had devastated Southern Phaidhodor and ravaged Raven Rock to the west. Neloth dedicated his life to researching the Heart Stone and the Ash Spawn being created from the Tuathon's mystical ash. He eventually became interested in Daedric Artifacts affiliated to Hermaeus Mora, the Daedric Prince of Knowledge. The Black Books were sought out by Neloth and he even discovered the lost Tekreir city of Nchardak.
